## Andrew Aziz — *How to Day Trade for a Living* (2015/2020)

**Thesis:** Day trading is a profession. Edge = few mastered setups + ≤2% risk + discipline. Trade catalyst-driven “Stocks in Play,” not hope.

**Risk (hard numbers):**
- Max **2%** account risk/trade (often 1%)
- Min **2:1** reward:risk
- Aziz himself: ~**30% of trades lose** and he stays profitable via R:R
- Tiny accounts cannot fund big daily income ($5k ≠ $2k/day)
- US PDT **$25k** floor for frequent day trades

**Nine setups (PlayBook):** ABCD, Bull Flag, Bottom/Top Reversal, MA trend, VWAP, Horizontal S/R, Red↔Green, Opening Range Breakout. Shared: technical stops, no average-down, partials → BE → trail.

**For Aegis:** Steal risk algebra, named strategies, ORB/VWAP/S&R/flag patterns, session gates, journaling. Drop equity Level 2, float, PDT, chatroom alpha.

## Steidlmayer / Hawkins — *Steidlmayer on Markets* (2003)

**Thesis:** Markets are auctions. **Price + Time = Value**. Market Profile shows acceptance (horizontal) vs rejection (vertical). Diagnose *present* conditions — do not predict like classic TA.

**Core objects:** TPO letters, Initial Balance (1st hour), value area (~68%), POC, day types (normal / trend / neutral…), initiating vs responsive vs prior value, four steps of market activity, auction points / single prints.

**Trading:** Manage by structure; size by opportunity quality; trend days go-with and often exit by close; fade failed extensions; no holy grail; capital safety first.

**For Aegis:** Session Profile / IB / day-type state machine from intraday OHLCV; structural S/R from auction points. Needs 30m (or session) bars; true CBOT LDB/OFI not available on Mac yfinance FX/BTC.
