The design burns its own evidence: using the sealed holdout as the adoption arbiter contaminates it — once this gate's result selects or rejects the challenger, the holdout is spent, and any future challengers compared on the same window inherit selection bias. "Reduces false-positive trades" is unmeasurable as stated (there's no ground-truth label for a "false positive" entry), and since hysteresis mechanically shrinks trade count, both the expectancy improvement and the 10%-drawdown allowance are noise-dominated at small n with no minimum-sample, bootstrap, or significance requirement. The free parameters — two consecutive observations, 10% drawdown tolerance — appear asserted rather than swept, and there's no walk-forward or multi-regime check, so a single-window pass is indistinguishable from regime luck. Finally, the proposal omits required governance: no ExperimentRegistry entry specified, no commitment to promotion solely via `bot/scripts/research_promote_champion.py` after all `promote.py` gates, and "net of spread and slippage" names no cost model — whose assumptions govern the arithmetic.
