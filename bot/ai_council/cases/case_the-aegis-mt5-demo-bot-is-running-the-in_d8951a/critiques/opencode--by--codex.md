The 60% analogue-confidence threshold and 10% expectancy-improvement cutoff are arbitrary and may be tuned to the sealed holdout, creating overfitting and invalidating its independence. “Modeled” spread/slippage and “net expectancy” are not defined or required to be calibrated against realized execution costs, while maximum drawdown and trade-count changes lack statistical uncertainty, minimum sample size, and regime-wise validation.
