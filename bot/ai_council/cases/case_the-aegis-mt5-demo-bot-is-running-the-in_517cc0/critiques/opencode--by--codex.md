The rolling 75th-percentile threshold is underspecified: window length, minimum sample size, session definition, and whether the current spread is included could introduce leakage or unstable, overfit cutoffs. “Improves holdout expectancy” and “without increasing maximum drawdown” lack uncertainty bounds, economic significance thresholds, and multiple-testing controls, so a noisy improvement could pass. The proposal also does not explicitly prohibit live-path/config changes or require verifying that spread costs are not double-counted against the existing net-cost assumptions.
