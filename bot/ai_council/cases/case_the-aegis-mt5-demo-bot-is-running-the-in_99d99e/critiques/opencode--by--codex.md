The 90th-percentile threshold, rolling window, ATR timing, and suppression rules are underspecified, creating substantial degrees of freedom for overfitting. Repeatedly evaluating or tuning on the sealed holdout would contaminate it; acceptance also lacks minimum sample size, confidence intervals, and multiple-testing correction, so a 0.05R improvement and unchanged drawdown may be noise. The proposal does not require ExperimentRegistry recording or explicitly prohibit live deployment/config changes, and the arbitrary 70% trade-count floor is not justified by statistical power or risk objectives.
