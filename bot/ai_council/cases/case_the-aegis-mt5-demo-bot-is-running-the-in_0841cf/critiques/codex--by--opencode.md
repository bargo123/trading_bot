The zero-EV admission bar filters noise, not edge: with no margin above costs, no confidence interval on expectancy, and only ~30 signals, "higher net expectancy" is a point estimate indistinguishable from luck—an unmeasurable claim absent a significance test or bootstrap. "No worse maximum drawdown" compares a single-path statistic with huge variance; without a defined tolerance or distributional comparison, near-ties are arbitrary. Reusing the same sealed holdout for every successive challenger burns it via repeated peeking, and there is no multiple-comparison control despite an implied stream of challengers. Finally, nothing specifies how "estimated EV" and "validated state" are computed—if the EV model or validation labels touch the evaluation window, the entire test is lookahead-contaminated before it starts.
