The 60-second threshold is arbitrary and risks overfitting to the sealed logs’ latency and regime characteristics without testing other thresholds or unseen regimes. “Lower net adverse excursion” and “no more than 5% reduction” are underspecified: no sample-size/power requirement, confidence intervals, aggregation rule, or handling of multiple comparisons is given. Historical DEMO logs also cannot validate live-feed behavior, clock failures, missing quotes, or fail-safe handling, so the proposal does not establish operational safety.
