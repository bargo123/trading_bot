“Validated-state” is undefined, and adding three confirmations can simply reduce exposure and cherry-pick fewer trades rather than improve signal quality; the 10% expectancy threshold is also ambiguous and lacks statistical uncertainty, minimum-trade, and multiple-testing controls. A single sealed historical window does not establish robustness across regimes or a truly untouched out-of-sample period, and the 5% drawdown/trade-count limits can hide harmful changes in timing, tail loss, and opportunity cost.
