The gate thresholds (quote age ≤1s, spread < 30-day median) are arbitrary constants presented without prior tuning discipline, and the 30-day median must be computed as a strictly trailing window inside the replay — any full-sample or centered computation is silent lookahead on exactly the variable being gated. The acceptance test is statistically weak and self-undermining: overlapping/non-overlapping confidence intervals are a notoriously poor significance test, and since the stricter gate mechanically shrinks the trade count, wider CIs from fewer trades make "non-overlapping" nearly unfalsifiable while per-trade expectancy says nothing about aggregate PnL; use a paired bootstrap over matched trade timestamps instead. "No material increase in tail loss" is unmeasurable as written — name the metric and delta (e.g., CVaR95 or max drawdown within X% of baseline) or the gate is decorative. Finally, every ablation run against the same sealed replay burns its seal through repeated looks, and a single time-ordered DEMO path proves nothing about regime robustness (stale-quote conditions cluster in rollover/news windows the sample may barely contain), so require pre-registered one-shot evaluation plus a validation-split pass before the sealed set is ever touched.
