The proposal's headline claim — reducing "false starts" — is unmeasurable as written because neither "false start" nor "validated state" is operationally defined, leaving no computable metric. The acceptance rule is both gameable and overfit-prone: a 10% relative expectancy gain with only ≤5% allowed trade-count decline can be trivially met by abstaining more (fewer trades mechanically shrinks drawdown and inflates per-trade expectancy), there is no minimum sample size or significance test so a handful of lucky trades qualifies, "three consecutive confirmations" is a magic number with no 2-vs-3-vs-4 sensitivity analysis, and relative-only thresholds ignore absolute net expectancy — a candidate could pass while making less money overall than the incumbent. Validation is underspecified: "sealed MT5 DEMO historical windows" are contaminated unless they provably exclude every period that shaped the current gate's parameters, and no walk-forward or unseal-once-after-pre-registration protocol is stated. Safety compliance is otherwise sound (registry recording, champion gates untouched, DEMO-only), contingent on pre-registering the exact confirmation definition, false-start metric, minimum trade count, and an absolute performance floor before any sealed window is opened.
