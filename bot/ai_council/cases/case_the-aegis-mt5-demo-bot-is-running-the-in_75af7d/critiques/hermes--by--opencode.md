The proposal risks overfitting promotion criteria to a tiny set of historical promotions; one failure does not establish that split gating improves out-of-sample performance. “Both splits” is undefined without fixed, pre-registered split boundaries and sufficient trade counts, and positive EV alone ignores uncertainty, drawdown, and multiple-testing effects. Recomputing sealed holdouts must preserve their immutability and avoid using results to tune gates before the claimed prospective confirmation.
