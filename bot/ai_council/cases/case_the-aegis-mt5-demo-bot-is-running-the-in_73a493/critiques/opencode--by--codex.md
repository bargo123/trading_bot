The 70th-percentile threshold and 10% improvement criterion appear arbitrary and invite threshold overfitting, while “validated-state” and the percentile lookback/reference population are undefined. Reusing sealed holdouts to accept or reject the filter compromises their independence; there is no untouched confirmation set, walk-forward validation, regime analysis, or statistical significance/multiple-testing control. The proposal also omits risk-adjusted and drawdown metrics, and “record the result” does not explicitly require the mandated ExperimentRegistry.
