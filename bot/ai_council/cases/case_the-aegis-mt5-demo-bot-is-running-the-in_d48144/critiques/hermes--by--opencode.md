The cutoff sweep risks overfitting the sealed holdout unless calibration data, cutoff selection, and final evaluation are strictly separated. “Posterior margin” is unvalidated unless the validator’s probabilities are demonstrably calibrated by regime and market period; a high margin can still be confidently wrong. The adoption criterion omits sample-size, multiple-testing correction, and a forward/chronological out-of-sample confirmation, so 95% bootstrap confidence alone may not establish robustness.
