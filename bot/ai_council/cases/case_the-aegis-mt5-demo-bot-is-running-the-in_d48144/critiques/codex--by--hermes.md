The (3-bar, +0.25R) trigger pair are unjustified magic numbers with no stated selection provenance or sensitivity sweep, so a single sealed-holdout draw risks confirming a threshold eyeballed from overlapping development data rather than a real edge — and "three completed bars" never names the timeframe, making the rule unmeasurable as written. The acceptance criterion "improves expectancy without worsening tail loss" has no numeric margin, minimum trade count, significance test, or defined tail statistic (max DD? CVaR? worst-N?), which lets noise pass as signal since early exits concentrate outcomes into a few survivors. Missing validations: cost drag from doubling round-trip frequency, opportunity-cost blindness toward trades that would have run past +0.25R later, a trivially inflated scratch rate that confounds the metric set (the challenger mechanically relabels losers as scratches), interaction/duplication with the existing rapid-invalidation exit path, verification that the holdout is uncontaminated by the baseline's own tuning history, and no mention of ExperimentRegistry registration required before any run. Safety-wise the proposal stays inside DEMO/research bounds and defers promotion to existing gates, but any DEMO-side trial of this exit must flow solely through the designated runner under full governance — nothing here may be wired into live behavior outside the promote.py path.
