A single 12-bar horizon risks overfitting, especially if the sealed holdout is queried repeatedly; the holdout must remain untouched until the specification is frozen. “Improves net expectancy” lacks a statistical uncertainty threshold and minimum trade/sample-size requirement, while maximum drawdown and losing-streak comparisons are highly noisy. The proposal also does not require walk-forward or regime-stratified validation, nor explicitly verify that exit features use only information available at the decision time.
