The proposal assumes analogue matches are genuinely independent without defining or testing independence, inviting correlated historical regimes to masquerade as confirmation. “Materially” and “does not worsen” lack pre-registered effect sizes, confidence intervals, and sample-size requirements, making rejection criteria discretionary. It also omits multiple-testing controls and an explicit frozen holdout protocol, so iterative gate tuning can overfit the sealed holdout despite the stated promotion restraint.
