The proposal's acceptance criteria are unfalsifiable as written: "net expectancy improves" specifies no minimum margin or confidence bound (the repo already requires positive OOS confidence bounds for promotion, which this omits), and "trade count remains sufficient for statistical comparison" has no numeric floor, power calculation, or accounting for the heavy trade overlap between the two gates — which makes naive count-based comparison invalid since outcomes are paired and correlated. The EV component of the filter is underspecified: where does the positive-EV threshold come from, and is the below-median spread percentile computed over a causal rolling window or one that peeks at future ticks? Worse, conditioning entry on spread state silently selects low-volatility sessions, so any lift may be regime selection rather than added edge — require a per-session/per-volatility breakdown before crediting the filter. Finally, nothing here commits to single-shot holdout discipline (iteratively re-testing variants against the same sealed set erodes it into a second training set) or names ExperimentRegistry registration and the promote.py gates, so as stated it bypasses required governance.
