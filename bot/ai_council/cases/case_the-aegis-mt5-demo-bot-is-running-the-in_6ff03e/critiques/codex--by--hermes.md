The 1.5× multiple and the 5% precision tolerance are free parameters with no stated provenance or sensitivity analysis — tune them post hoc against the same holdouts and you've built an overfitting machine dressed as a gate. "Improves net expectancy and drawdown" is unmeasurable as written: no baseline comparison, no minimum effect size, no drawdown definition, no holdout length, and "statistically consistent across instruments and market regimes" names neither a test nor a regime taxonomy — leaving regimes undefined until after results is textbook subgroup cherry-picking. Worse, abstention mechanically trades less, so precision can improve trivially while expectancy CIs widen into meaninglessness; the proposal needs pre-registered thresholds, sample-size floors, and multiplicity correction across instruments before any pass/fail claim is falsifiable. Finally, it never states costs are computed under the runner's actual live spread/slippage assumptions versus historical averages — a biased-low cost estimate silently invalidates every EV ratio downstream, and there's no mention of recording the trial in ExperimentRegistry as governance requires.
