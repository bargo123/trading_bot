The proposal risks overfitting to a small, selectively logged set of realized DEMO trades and does not specify reconciliation rules for partial fills, commissions, spread snapshots, missing records, or broker-side execution differences. Its ±0.1R tolerance is arbitrary and the “previously rejected challenger would have passed” comparison can become post-hoc selection unless candidates, cost mappings, and decision thresholds are frozen before any data access. It also lacks uncertainty estimates, minimum sample/power requirements, and an independent untouched validation period; sealed-holdout data must not be used to tune or choose the next gating change.
