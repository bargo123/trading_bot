The 1.5x threshold is an arbitrary hyperparameter unless selected without tuning on the sealed holdouts, and multiple threshold/regime/instrument comparisons create material selection-bias risk. “Statistically consistent,” “market regimes,” net expectancy, and drawdown need pre-registered definitions, sample-size/power requirements, confidence intervals, and a correction for multiple testing. Estimated execution costs must be validated against realized fills and stress-tested for spread spikes; otherwise the gate may optimize against stale or understated costs.
