Calibrate the intelligent firehose Stop Loss (SL) to the 95th percentile of measured Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) observed in the winning population, replacing the current 30-pip formula. Measured evidence from the recent outcome learning cycle identifies SL exits as highly destructive (−0.503 average), while Tharp (*Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom*) advocates for stops derived from empirical excursion rather than arbitrary distances. This modification will test whether tightening the risk to the observed 'pain threshold' of successful trades improves the Profit Factor more than it decays the win rate. This research candidate specifically targets the 'cost mirage' identified in recent ML pipeline findings by aligning the risk profile with validated price action behavior.
