Calibrating to the 95th-percentile MAE of winners is survivorship/selection bias: it excludes losers and may simply encode the historical regime, instrument, and entry logic rather than a transferable pain threshold. “Highly destructive” SL exits and improved Profit Factor are unmeasurable without defined samples, costs, confidence intervals, out-of-sample or walk-forward validation, and comparison against the existing rule under identical execution assumptions. Tightening SL based on winners can increase premature exits and tail losses, and replacing the live formula or testing through the broker runner without explicit governed approval would violate the protected-config and DEMO-only execution controls.
