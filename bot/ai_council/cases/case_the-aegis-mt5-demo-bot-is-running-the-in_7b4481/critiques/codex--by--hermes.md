The 1.5× multiplier is an unjustified free parameter: if it was scanned or tuned against the same sealed observations used to judge the challenger, that's in-sample selection, and the proposal never commits to fixing it ex ante plus validating out-of-sample across regimes/sessions rather than one historical path. The promotion claim is unmeasurable as stated — "improves net expectancy and drawdown" specifies no effect size, significance test, or minimum sample, and with up to 10% fewer eligible signals any expectancy delta will be small-N noise while raw max-drawdown comparisons are mechanically biased downward simply because the challenger trades less. The cost term is underspecified: it must state whether "measured spread-plus-slippage" uses point-in-time runner assumptions or period averages, since averaging understates precisely the tail-spread episodes this gate exists to avoid, violating the net-of-costs requirement. Finally, promotion cannot be granted on two metrics alone — the challenger must pass every gate in bot/aegis/research/promote.py, be registered in the ExperimentRegistry, and the sealed set must be verified uncontaminated by any data that informed the current champion's gate.
