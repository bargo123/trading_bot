The assumption that historical "realized outcomes" provide sufficient sample density for a Fisher exact p < 0.05 is mathematically optimistic and likely unmeasurable given the limited promotion history in `ExperimentRegistry`. Block-bootstrap CIs introduce an arbitrary block-size parameter that is prone to overfitting and can be tuned to selectively exclude valid alpha under the guise of "sampling noise" reduction. Additionally, the proposal ignores that a 95% CI is valid only under stationarity, meaning it will systematically fail to protect against the regime shifts that actually cause post-promotion expectancy collapse.
