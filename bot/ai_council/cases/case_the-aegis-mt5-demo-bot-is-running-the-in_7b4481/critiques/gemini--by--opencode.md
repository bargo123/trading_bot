The winning-trade MAE distribution is selection-biased and may set stops too tight for trades that would recover, especially across changing volatility regimes. Claims about “validated price action” and fixing a “cost mirage” are unmeasurable without a preregistered, cost-inclusive holdout comparison against the 30-pip baseline. The proposal also omits walk-forward validation, sample size/regime breakdowns, and safeguards against silently increasing tail-loss or execution risk.
