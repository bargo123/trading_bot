The proposal risks overfitting the promotion rule to a retrospective comparison on the same sealed holdout, effectively consuming or contaminating the holdout before prospective validation. Its target of Fisher p < 0.05 is underpowered and vulnerable to multiple-comparison and selection effects, while “nominal rate” coverage is not a clear falsifiable benchmark for overlapping, nonstationary trades or data-dependent horizons. It also fails to specify minimum sample sizes, bootstrap methodology, challenger independence, and an untouched forward test, so any apparent improvement could be sampling noise rather than safer promotion.
