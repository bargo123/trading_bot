Revised proposal: Extend the validated-state gate in `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` to require the lower bound of a 95% stationary-block-bootstrap confidence interval (expected block length fixed from each candidate's own logged trade durations, horizons taken exactly as already recorded in `ExperimentRegistry` — nothing tuned post hoc) to be strictly above zero on the sealed holdout net of runner spread/slippage, with the retrospective pre-registered before unblinding: a minimum of 40 sealed challenger decisions (below which the test is declared underpowered and abandoned on record), one hypothesis tested via a regime-stratified permutation test rather than Fisher exact, and a single execution of the analysis whose result — pass or fail — is written to the registry either way. Because realized post-promotion outcomes are confounded by evolving execution, portfolio, and selection rules, the claim is narrowed from causation to discrimination — whether the CI lower-bound sign at decision time predicts subsequent negative net expectancy within the already-recorded cohort — and the stationarity objection is accepted outright: the CI gate guards sampling noise only, so it is proposed strictly as a necessary, not sufficient, condition, complemented by a walk-forward, regime-partitioned check rather than presented as regime-shift protection. To avoid consuming the holdout, the retrospective touches only decisions logged before today and the gate change itself would be adopted solely after a shadow phase in which both gates are scored on new untouched challengers for a pre-committed window (at least 3 months or 20 decisions) while promotion remains governed exclusively by the current point-estimate gate. The switch proceeds only if the shadow phase shows the CI gate would have blocked every subsequently failed promotion while vetoing none that later succeeded; otherwise this candidate dies on the record.
