This proposal treats the sealed holdout as a reusable tuning target: any candidate gated on "≥10% expectancy improvement" invites iterate-and-resubmit leakage, so thresholds must be pre-registered and the unseal must be strictly one-shot with no re-evaluation after failure. The criteria are also statistically vacuous — no confidence interval or bootstrap on a likely small-sample expectancy delta, "drawdown worse by 5%" is undefined (relative vs. absolute), and per-trade expectancy plus a floor of only 80% trade count means aggregate expectancy can collapse while all gates pass; require total net-of-cost PnL under the runner's own spread/slippage assumptions. The walk-forward is asserted but unspecified — fold count, purge/embargo between segments, and critically whether the added closed-bar confirmation delays entry fills by one full bar in the backtest (otherwise it's textbook lookahead) are all unstated. Compliance is nominally fine (registry, no champion edit), but the spec must land in `bot/research/strategies/` and the outcome recorded even if rejected — silence on rejection handling is how failed ablations get quietly rerun.
