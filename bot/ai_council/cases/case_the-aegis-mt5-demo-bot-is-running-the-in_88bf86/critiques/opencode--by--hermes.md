"Adverse-entry rate" is never operationalized (what horizon, what measurement basis defines an entry as adverse?), so the 10% improvement bar is unmeasurable and unfalsifiable, and with no sample-size or significance requirement a thin replay slice can pass on noise. Worse, freshness gating depends on when evidence actually arrived at evaluation time, but historical validation/outcome logs are typically stamped post-hoc — unless replay reconstructs true decision-time evidence availability, the test embeds lookahead that flatters the challenger; and since the gate works by abstention, per-trade expectancy is the wrong metric — require whole-portfolio net PnL against the unchanged baseline, not per-trade averages over a self-selected surviving trade set. The 60s cutoff and the 10%/5% accept bands are arbitrary point estimates with no sensitivity sweep (e.g., 45–120s) or second-holdout confirmation, inviting threshold overfitting, and nothing specifies behavior during natural evidence gaps (Asia lull, weekend rollover) where systematic abstention could quietly reshape session exposure rather than merely filter bad entries. Finally, the proposal omits registration in ExperimentRegistry and a paper-shadow forward check before any promotion — both mandatory under house rules.
