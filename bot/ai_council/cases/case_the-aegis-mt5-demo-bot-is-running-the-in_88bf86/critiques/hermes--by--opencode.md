Shadow-replaying alternate scratches from logged closes is not a valid counterfactual unless intratrade quote paths, executable bid/ask fills, slippage, and competing exit precedence are recorded; otherwise both expectancy and adverse-excursion claims are unmeasurable. Testing only ±50% thresholds invites calibration overfitting, and a one-spread-unit hurdle without confidence intervals, sample-size requirements, or multiple-comparison control is arbitrary. A 95th-percentile metric can hide extreme losses, so require tail-loss/CVaR, regime-stratified sealed holdouts, and explicit treatment of trades that never reached scratch.
