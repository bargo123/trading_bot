ATR windows add tunable thresholds that can easily overfit the sealed holdout unless all parameters and selection rules are frozen before evaluation. “Expected move” and “statistical confidence” are unmeasurable without specifying their estimator, confidence test, sample-size/power requirement, and multiple-comparison controls. The proposal also lacks regime, symbol, and out-of-sample stability checks, so a lower drawdown could be a transient artifact rather than a robust improvement.
