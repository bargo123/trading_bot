The recency-weighting scheme is entirely unspecified—half-life, decay form, and hyperparameters are left free, guaranteeing a search over variants whose repeated evaluations quietly burn the "untouched" holdout; without a one-look rule and pre-registration of the exact weights before unsealing, the 10%/5% thresholds are tuned-on-the-test-set by construction. Neither improvement claim is statistically grounded: 10% net expectancy gain is stated without relative-vs-absolute definition, minimum sample size, confidence interval, or significance test (drawdown deltas are notoriously noise-dominated), and "false-positive entries" has no operational definition against which a 5% reduction could even be measured. Validation is incomplete—there's no walk-forward/CV requirement before the holdout, no confirmation that features derived from `outcome_log.jsonl` don't overlap the holdout period (data contamination via prior experiment logging), and simultaneous testing of two metrics invites multiplicity with no correction. Safety compliance is superficially adequate (registry, no promotion), but the sealing provenance—who sealed the holdout, when, and whether the sealer saw prior results—is unstated, which is exactly where leakage hides.
