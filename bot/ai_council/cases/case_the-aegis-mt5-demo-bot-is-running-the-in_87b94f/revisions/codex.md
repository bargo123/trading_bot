Pre-register one fixed candidate before unsealing: exponential weights \(w_i=2^{-\mathrm{age}_i/30\text{ days}}\), with a 30-day half-life and no tunable hyperparameters; evaluate it once against the current gate control. Before holdout access, require five chronological walk-forward folds, an audit proving that features and labels—including `outcome_log.jsonl` records—end before the holdout start, and registry metadata naming the sealer, UTC seal time, data hash, commit, and attestation that no prior results were seen. On a holdout containing at least 200 eligible entries, define false positives as entries with a cost-adjusted non-positive outcome and require a relative net-expectancy gain of at least 10% and relative false-positive-rate reduction of at least 5%, with both co-primary tests passing Bonferroni-adjusted one-sided \(\alpha=.025\) confidence bounds; require the one-sided 95% bound for maximum-drawdown change to show no worsening. Record the single holdout evaluation and all confidence intervals in the ExperimentRegistry, and promote nothing unless these criteria and every existing validation gate pass.
