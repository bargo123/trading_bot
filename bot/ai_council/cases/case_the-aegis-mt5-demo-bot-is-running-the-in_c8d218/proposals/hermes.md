Proposal: Add a realized-spread-regime check to the validated-state gate as a challenger tested in the next research cycle. Hypothesis: firehose entries admitted when the live DEMO spread at signal time exceeds the trailing 24-hour median by more than ~25% have negative net expectancy after the runner's existing spread/slippage cost model, because the gate currently validates market state but not executable-cost state. Falsification criterion: if the high-spread tercile's net-expectancy CI overlaps or beats the low-spread tercile's, reject the hypothesis and keep the gate unchanged.
