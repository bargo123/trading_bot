The ~200-trade, tercile-based test is underpowered and invites overfitting/p-hacking: the sample size, tercile cutoffs, and 25% threshold are not pre-registered, and “CI overlaps” is not a valid equivalence or superiority test. Conditioning only on gated-in trades creates selection bias, while signal-time spread may not represent executable/fill spread and may double-count costs already included in net expectancy; validation also lacks fixed out-of-sample periods, regime/asset stratification, and multiple-testing correction. The proposal should explicitly prove the logger is read-only and the single governed runner remains the sole MT5 mutator, with fail-closed behavior if spread data are missing or stale.
