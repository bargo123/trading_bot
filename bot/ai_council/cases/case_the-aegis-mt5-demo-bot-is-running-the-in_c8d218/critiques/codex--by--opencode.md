The 1.5x and 90th-percentile thresholds are arbitrary and invite tuning against the sealed holdout unless fixed using an earlier, strictly time-ordered validation set. “10% expectancy improvement” and “5% drawdown worsening” are undefined as relative versus absolute measures, and no confidence intervals, minimum sample size, or per-instrument/regime robustness test is required. A 30-minute median and realized-volatility percentile must be computed causally with explicit session, missing-data, and spread-cost handling, or the filter can leak future information and manufacture an apparent improvement.
