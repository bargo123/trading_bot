The 1.5×, 90th-percentile, and all three pass-gate numbers (10%/5%/70%) are unjustified hand-picked constants; if more than one configuration gets evaluated against the sealed holdout, the holdout is burned — thresholds must be tuned via in-sample walk-forward/CV, the exact spec registered in ExperimentRegistry before any holdout contact, and holdout access treated as strictly one-shot. Both filters risk lookahead as written: the spread median must be trailing-only, and "realized volatility above its 90th percentile" is unmeasurable until the percentile window is specified — a full-sample percentile leaks future information into every entry decision. Comparing expectancy and drawdown on a refiltered trade population is not apples-to-apples, since blocking high-vol regimes mechanically selects calmer trades (regime selection, not demonstrated edge), and a 10% expectancy delta on ~30% fewer trades has wide confidence intervals with no significance test or bootstrap proposed. Finally, key definitions are missing: expectancy per-trade or aggregate and net of which spread/slippage model, and whether "drawdown does not worsen by more than 5%" is relative or absolute — without these the pass condition cannot be adjudicated objectively.
