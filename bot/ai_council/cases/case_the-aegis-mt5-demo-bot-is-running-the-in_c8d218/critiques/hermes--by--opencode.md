The fixed 25% threshold and tercile split are arbitrary and likely overfit to a small, regime-dependent sample of roughly 200 trades. It does not define spread normalization by instrument, session, volatility, or quote quality, so the observed relationship may merely proxy adverse market conditions rather than executable cost. The proposal also needs a pre-registered minimum effect size, bootstrap method, and leakage-safe sealed-holdout evaluation before treating an exploratory DEMO result as a gate candidate.
