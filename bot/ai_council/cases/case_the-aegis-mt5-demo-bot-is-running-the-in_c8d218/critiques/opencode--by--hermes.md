The filter's free parameters — the 15-minute timeframe, the 60-bar lookback, the median threshold, and the 10% adoption bar — are stated without pre-registration or justification, which is exactly how tuning-on-the-holdout happens; and a single sealed-holdout pass with no confidence interval means a 10% expectancy delta on ~100 trades can be pure sampling noise.
2. "Post-entry volatility-expansion" used to reject entries is internally contradictory — the evaluation point (pre-entry ATR state vs. realized post-entry expansion) is undefined, so the claim isn't measurable as written.
3. Conditioning on trades that pass an ATR floor silently reweights the sample toward specific sessions and regimes, so the expectancy/drawdown comparison versus the unchanged gate isn't apples-to-apples even at n≥100 — and there's no power analysis behind that floor anyway.
4. Missing validation: no temporal or regime-stratified resampling, no walk-forward confirmation, no stated seal-integrity check on the holdout or ExperimentRegistry entry, and any adoption must still clear the governed `promote.py` gates rather than this proposal's self-declared criteria.
