The “15-minute post-entry” ATR filter is causally ambiguous: using information after entry to reject that entry introduces lookahead bias unless the trade is delayed or exited under a pre-specified rule. The 60-bar median and 10% improvement threshold appear arbitrary and invite overfitting, while a 100-trade minimum does not establish statistical significance or robustness across regimes, symbols, and time periods. It also omits confidence intervals, multiple-testing controls, and an explicit fail-closed governance check confirming the experiment cannot alter live or DEMO execution paths.
