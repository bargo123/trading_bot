The 1.25× threshold and 60-minute window are unexamined free parameters with no sensitivity analysis, and any re-tuning after seeing the sealed holdout result is holdout erosion by stealth — the protocol must be single-shot, pre-registered (thresholds frozen and logged in ExperimentRegistry before first seal contact), or it's fitting the holdout. The acceptance metrics are under-specified and gameable: "10% net expectancy improvement" never states relative vs absolute, a minimum trade count, or a significance test, and a filter that rejects many signals will almost mechanically lower max drawdown and inflate per-trade expectancy while destroying total PnL — so both gates are biased toward passing noise unless you add bootstrap CIs on the expectancy delta plus an explicit total-PnL/opportunity-cost non-degradation condition. Validation is also incomplete: there's no out-of-time or regime check that the sealed window's spread distribution (session/news-dependent, and undefined as to tick- vs bar-sampled median) resembles live conditions, and no interaction test confirming the filter composes cleanly with the existing quote/economics gates rather than only beating the baseline in isolation. Governance-wise nothing violates the read-only rules and retaining the baseline on failure is right, but "accept" must mean routing the challenger through research_promote_champion.py and every promote.py gate — the proposal's private pass/fail criterion cannot substitute for the governed promotion path.
