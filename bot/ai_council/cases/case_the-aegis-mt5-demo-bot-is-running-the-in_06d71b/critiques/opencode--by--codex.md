The 1.25 threshold and 60-minute window appear arbitrary and invite overfitting unless pre-registered and tested across symbols, regimes, and untouched time periods; the rolling median must explicitly exclude the current observation and handle sparse or stale quotes. “Net expectancy improves by 10%” is unmeasurable without a minimum trade count, confidence intervals, statistical test, and precise expectancy definition, while “no increase in maximum drawdown” needs a tolerance for sampling noise. The proposal also omits mandatory ExperimentRegistry recording and governed promotion gates, and does not establish that spread/slippage assumptions are conservative or independently validated.
