“Validated-state” and “false start” are undefined, so the claimed improvement cannot be measured reproducibly or distinguished from simply delaying entries and missing profitable moves. A single sealed holdout is vulnerable to regime dependence and selection bias, while “lower maximum drawdown” plus “non-inferior expectancy” lacks pre-specified thresholds, power analysis, confidence intervals, and an appropriate paired statistical test. The proposal also omits adverse execution, signal-latency, skipped-trade opportunity cost, and live DEMO shadow validation, so unchanged sizing/exits do not establish that the gate is safe or robust.
