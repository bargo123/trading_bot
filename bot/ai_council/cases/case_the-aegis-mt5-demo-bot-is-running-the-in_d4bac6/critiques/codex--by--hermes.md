Reusing the sealed holdout for this A/B is flawed twice over: the incumbent champion was presumably validated on that same window, so the comparison is biased toward the gate already fitted to it, and every additional candidate replayed against a "sealed" holdout erodes the seal — by proposal N it's just another training set; demand a fresh untouched holdout or a pre-registered single-use budget. One replay path yields a point estimate, not evidence: without a paired bootstrap (or block bootstrap honoring trade overlap) plus a minimum-trade-count floor, claimed improvements in net expectancy and drawdown are indistinguishable from noise, and drawdown differences especially need far longer samples than any single sealed window. "Trade-validity calibration beyond predefined tolerances" is unfalsifiable as written — the numeric tolerance must be committed to the experiment registry before the run, alongside the exact margin-calibration grid; if more than one margin value was tried anywhere in the pipeline, the grid size and selection rule must be disclosed or the "calibrated only on training data" claim is unverifiable. Finally, spread-normalizing the margin at decision time makes the gate endogenous to regime-dependent spread widening (exactly when uncertainty spikes), so validate the candidate's calibration separately across session/regime strata, not just pooled.
