The ~50 fills per symbol are a small, regime- and execution-condition-biased sample; medians lack uncertainty and ignore tail costs, fees, latency, partial fills, and spread variation. “Requested versus filled” prices do not by themselves isolate spread from slippage, and rescoring the sealed holdout with post-hoc realized-fill data risks holdout contamination and lookahead. Making a noisy calibration mandatory changes a governed promotion gate, so it requires registry-recorded validation, confidence bounds, an untouched out-of-sample period, and explicit authorization rather than being treated as a purely research-only change.
