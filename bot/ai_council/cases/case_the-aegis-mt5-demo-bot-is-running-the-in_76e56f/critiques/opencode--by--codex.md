The proposal risks overfitting by tuning an EV margin and judging success on a single sealed holdout, while the arbitrary 10% improvement and unchanged maximum drawdown thresholds lack statistical justification. Spread/slippage estimates may be optimistic or regime-specific, and “validated-state signal” and “net expectancy improves” are insufficiently defined for reproducible measurement, including confidence intervals and minimum trade count. It also omits robustness checks across instruments, time periods, and stress-cost scenarios, so a holdout win could be noise rather than a safe improvement.
