The 12% Profit Factor target and 300-trade threshold are arbitrary without pre-registered confidence intervals, regime-stratified holdouts, or multiple-testing controls, inviting overfitting. ATR is an indirect proxy for tick velocity and slippage; the proposal must measure actual executable spread/slippage and define the data source, latency, and cost model. It also improperly proposes modifying a validated-state gate before isolated research validation, and “price-action clarity” is an unmeasurable post-hoc claim.
