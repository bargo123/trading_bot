Replaying the already-sealed holdout is textbook holdout contamination—those labels were consumed validating the champion, so any "improvement" measured there is selection noise, not evidence, and repeated replays across challengers demand a multiple-testing correction this proposal lacks. The 10%-expectancy bar carries no confidence interval or significance test, so you'd be promoting on point estimates from a single parameter perturbation; worse, raising the sample threshold changes *which* trades qualify, meaning the challenger is compared against champion results computed on a different trade population—an apples-to-oranges comparison that also makes the 70% trade-count floor ill-defined. Finally, "accept" as defined here substitutes ad-hoc criteria for the governed gates in `bot/aegis/research/promote.py`, which is a governance violation regardless of whether the numbers look good.
