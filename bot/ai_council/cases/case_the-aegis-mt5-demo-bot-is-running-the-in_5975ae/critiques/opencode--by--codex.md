The cap is calibrated from the same observed outcomes (“~2× average win” and worst-decile losses), creating material selection and overfitting risk unless its value is frozen using training folds only. “Without materially cutting” win rate is undefined, and aggregate expectancy/PF alone omit confidence intervals, regime stability, sample size per fold, and sensitivity testing across cap values. Most importantly, exit truncation cannot be validated from reconciled final exits unless intratrade paths and executable stop fills are available; otherwise the claimed capped outcomes are counterfactual, potentially look-ahead-biased, and not genuinely net of slippage.
