The proposal commits a causal leap: profitable TP exits do not prove unattributed exits caused the losses, since close reason is confounded with trade quality and adverse price paths. Replaying and tuning the time-stop on the same 2,518-exit window invites overfitting, while “MFE ≥ average win” is ambiguous and potentially look-ahead-contaminated; the sealed holdout must remain untouched until the rule and threshold are frozen, then be evaluated on independent temporal folds with uncertainty and minimum-sample gates. The claim that relaxing one parameter will flip expectancy is also unsupported without modeling the post-stop path, added exposure, tail risk, and realistic costs. Finally, an “≥80% explained” attribution threshold measures labeling coverage, not attribution accuracy, and needs validation against an independently audited sample.
