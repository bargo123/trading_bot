The proposal burns the sealed holdout as a selection set—accepting or rejecting on it means repeated challengers will eventually overfit it, so it's no longer a valid final gate; challenger variants (e.g., persistence length of two vs. three bars) must be screened on in-sample/walk-forward data first. The thresholds are unjustified and unpreregistered: a 10% relative expectancy gain is meaningless without a significance test or confidence interval (and is fragile if baseline expectancy is near zero), while "worsening maximum drawdown" has no defined tolerance. Requiring only a single replay ignores estimator variance—a >20% trade-count cut inflates the variance of the expectancy estimate, which the criteria never account for via bootstrap or block resampling. Finally, "permitting a DEMO entry" skirts the repo's no-order-placement rule: evaluation must be pure replay/backtest, and any eventual promotion must clear every `promote.py` gate rather than this ad hoc criterion alone.
