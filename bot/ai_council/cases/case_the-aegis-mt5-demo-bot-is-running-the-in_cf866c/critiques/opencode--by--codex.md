Using the sealed holdout to choose or accept the 0.65 threshold leaks evaluation data and risks overfitting; the threshold must be fixed before a fresh holdout test. “Model confidence” needs a defined, calibrated measurement, and “net expectancy improves” must include uncertainty/significance and a predeclared horizon, not just a point estimate. The proposal also omits mandatory experiment-registry recording and does not specify how trade-count, spread/slippage, and every promotion gate will be independently audited without touching live configuration.
