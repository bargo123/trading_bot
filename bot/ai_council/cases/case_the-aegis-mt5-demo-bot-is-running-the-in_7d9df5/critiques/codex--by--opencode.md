The 15% ATR cost-abstention threshold is asserted, never derived—no pre-registration or sensitivity analysis, so it reads like a knob tuned on data overlapping the evaluation splits, and any post-hoc adjustment after seeing walk-forward results is leakage. Comparing expectancy between a strategy that abstains and one that doesn't is apples-to-oranges: fewer trades means higher-variance expectancy that can clear a 20% bar by luck, yet there's no minimum-trade-count floor, no significance test, no multiple-testing control, and "no worse max drawdown" with zero tolerance is a brittle knife-edge criterion. Nothing specifies the sealed holdout is one-shot; repeated challengers knocking on the same holdout guarantee eventual promotion of noise via selection bias. Finally, the proposal omits whether the challenger runs through every gate in `promote.py` and gets an `ExperimentRegistry` entry regardless of verdict—without those, the "record it" promise is unverifiable.
