The proposed 2–3× cap is tuned directly from the observed average win and tail-loss outcomes, creating substantial overfitting and potential label leakage; replaying the same exit window is not independent validation. The experiment is not reproducible without specifying the time-stop rule, intratrade price path, fill model, and treatment of existing manual/SL/TP exits—exit timestamps alone cannot establish whether the new stops would have triggered. A single PF ≥1.0 and win-rate threshold also ignores regime stability, uncertainty, drawdown, sample-size effects, and multiple-testing risk; citing trading books is not validation.
