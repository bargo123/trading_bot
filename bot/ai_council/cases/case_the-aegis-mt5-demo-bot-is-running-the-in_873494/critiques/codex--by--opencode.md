Flawed by construction: deriving the suppression threshold from the *current median* of the very holdout you then replay is in-sample threshold fitting — the filter's parameter is tuned on the test set, guaranteeing optimistic bias, and re-replaying an already-sealed holdout for yet another challenger compounds multiple-testing erosion of what little validity it had left. "Improve" carries no minimum effect size or significance test, profit factor and drawdown on ~30 retained trades are statistically meaningless, and "validated-state confidence" is undefined (median over which window, computed at what timestamp — entry-time features or peeking?). Finally, acceptance criteria are self-declared rather than routed through every gate in `promote.py`; per governance, any promotion must go via `research_promote_champion.py`'s full checks, not this ad-hoc criterion list — as written, this is reject-on-safety-procedure grounds regardless of results.
