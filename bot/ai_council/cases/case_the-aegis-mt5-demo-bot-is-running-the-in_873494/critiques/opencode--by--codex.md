The proposal risks selection and multiple-testing overfit because “stratified sample” and “cluster” definitions are unspecified, and searching many symbol×session×direction groups makes a nominal sealed holdout insufficient without multiplicity correction and untouched final validation. The ≥100-trade, +0.05R, and PF ≥1.3 thresholds lack power analysis, confidence bounds, regime stability tests, and an independent temporal holdout; one cycle may not contain enough representative gated-out outcomes to make the claimed bidirectional falsifiability measurable. It also omits explicit lookahead/audit checks proving counterfactual entries and exits were observable at decision time and does not state that every replay—including failures and rejected clusters—will be recorded in `ExperimentRegistry`, as required.
