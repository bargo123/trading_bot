The 14-period ATR percentile and top-20% cutoff are arbitrary and invite threshold overfitting without walk-forward, regime-stratified, and out-of-sample validation. A 15% Profit Factor target over only 500 backtest trades is insufficient unless costs, slippage, trade-count changes, drawdown, and statistical uncertainty are reported; filtering can improve PF simply by avoiding trades. The claim of reduced “churn” is unmeasurable without a defined churn metric and must not weaken existing DEMO execution, risk, or abstention gates.
