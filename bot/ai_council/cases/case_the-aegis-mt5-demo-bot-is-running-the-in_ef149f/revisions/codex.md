Test a frozen two-consecutive-evaluation validated-state gate using purged walk-forward validation, explicitly modeling the resulting signal and fill-timing delay in a separate replay. The primary objective is aggregate net PnL after spread/slippage; accept only if the candidate’s paired block-bootstrap 95% CI for the PnL difference versus the current gate is wholly above zero, with coverage no more than 2 percentage points lower, maximum drawdown no more than 5% higher, Brier score no more than 0.01 worse, and reliability slope remaining within 0.9–1.1. All thresholds, metrics, and the candidate-selection procedure must be preregistered in the ExperimentRegistry; challengers may iterate on walk-forward data, but the sealed holdout is evaluated once on the single frozen candidate (or under Holm correction for a preregistered challenger family). Promotion, if every gate passes, must occur only through `research_promote_champion.py`; otherwise retain the current champion and record the rejection and metrics.
