The acceptance criteria are unfalsifiable as written: "statistically supported," "materially," and "no deterioration" name no test, threshold, or equivalence margin for coverage, drawdown, or calibration (which metric — Brier, reliability slope?), inviting post-hoc judgment calls. Requiring two consecutive evaluations introduces signal lag whose fill-timing shift must be modeled separately from the gating effect, and per-trade expectancy can improve purely by shrinking trade count while aggregate net PnL falls — the objective is never pinned down. There is no mention of purged walk-forward validation before touching the sealed holdout, nor any multiple-comparison control despite repeated challenger testing on the same holdout, which quietly converts it into a tuning set. Finally, "accept the candidate" omits any commitment to the governed promotion path (`research_promote_champion.py` gates + ExperimentRegistry record), which is exactly how untracked overrides slip through.
