Accepting on a single ≥10% expectancy-per-signal delta with no confidence interval, minimum sample size, or significance test means you'd promote noise as readily as edge — and per-signal expectancy is trivially gamed: culling the worst 20% of trades mechanically raises it while total net PnL falls, so aggregate outcome and drawdown/tail behavior must be gated too. Every challenger evaluated against the same sealed holdout burns it through multiple testing; the 10%/20% thresholds must be pre-registered in the registry *before* unsealing, with an explicit budget on holdout reuses, or this is holdout-fitting dressed as governance. The persistence filter also silently shifts entry timing by one bar, changing both the traded population and realized spread/slippage exposure, so "same period and cost assumptions" does not make the comparison apples-to-apples unless entries are re-timestamped and re-costed. Finally, the proposal reads as if the holdout delta alone decides promotion — clarify that all standard `promote.py` gates (walk-forward stability, regime slices, cost-adjusted EV) still apply rather than being bypassed by this bespoke rule.
