The 15%/5% thresholds are bare round numbers with no derivation, no pre-registered minimum sample size, no confidence intervals, and no multiple-comparison control (e.g., tuning one-vs-three snapshots or the scratch definition until both gates pass), making them easy to satisfy by accident or overfit deliberately. The expectancy comparison is also endogenous: persistence filtering changes which trades become eligible, so any per-signal expectancy delta conflates filter quality with survivorship/selection effects, while "validated-state," snapshot cadence, and scratch classification (which depends on the same exit logic being tested) are left undefined and unreproducible. Validation is missing a sealed OOS holdout, a fixed evaluation window, statistical significance testing, and an ExperimentRegistry record, and "reject unless both thresholds are met without changing the champion" is ambiguous about whether the entry gate must touch the designated execution runner's protected YAML — a change that requires explicit operator authorization, not council approval alone.
