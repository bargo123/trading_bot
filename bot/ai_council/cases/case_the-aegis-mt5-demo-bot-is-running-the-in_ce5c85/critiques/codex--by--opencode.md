The proposal omits sample-size, confidence-interval, and regime-stratification requirements, so a 15% improvement could be noise or concentrated in a favorable market slice. “Invalidated or scratch trades” and “eligible signal” need fixed, pre-registered definitions, and expectancy must be net of spread, slippage, missed fills, and latency introduced by the second snapshot. DEMO shadow results cannot validate execution effects unless timestamps, quotes, and counterfactual fills are recorded against an unchanged control.
