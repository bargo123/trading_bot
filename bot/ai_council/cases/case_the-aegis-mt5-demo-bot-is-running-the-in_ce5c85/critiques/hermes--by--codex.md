“Realized” spread/slippage is not available at decision time, so using it risks lookahead and an EV estimate whose calibration, horizon, and cost model are unspecified. “Meaningful fraction” and “uplift” lack predefined effect-size and sample-size thresholds; replaying recent signals may also contaminate the sealed holdout, while ordinary bootstrap intervals ignore temporal dependence and repeated experimentation. The proposal does not specify multiple-testing controls, regime robustness, or safeguards proving the new abstention rule cannot alter governed execution outside the single DEMO owner.
