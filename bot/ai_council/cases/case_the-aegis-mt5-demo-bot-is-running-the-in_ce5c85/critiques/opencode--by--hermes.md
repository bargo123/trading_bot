The "two independent signal families" gate asserts independence it never tests — families trained on overlapping windows and features typically share failure modes, so agreement may be redundant correlation dressed as confirmation, and the family list/count must be frozen before evaluation. The acceptance criteria are unmeasurable as written: "reduces tail loss" names no statistic (CVaR at what quantile? max drawdown over what window?) and there's no minimum sample size or significance bar, so small-N holdout noise can either pass or veto the challenger arbitrarily. Validation is incomplete: recorded DEMO outcomes were generated under the champion's own entry distribution, so filtering that subset yields a selection-biased estimate of the filter's counterfactual effect, and nothing verifies the modeled spread/slippage matches realized fills before net-EV gating is trusted. Finally, nothing pre-registers the challenger variants or commits to ExperimentRegistry recording — without freezing the rule set before unsealing the holdout, repeated evaluation against the same sealed data is itself a leak channel and this becomes an overfitting engine rather than an experiment.
