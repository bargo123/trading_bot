“Realized” entry slippage may not be knowable at decision time, so the proposed net-EV feature risks leakage unless it uses only contemporaneous, pre-trade executable quotes and a pre-specified slippage model. A single recent replay and bootstrap interval do not establish robustness across symbols, sessions, regimes, or sparse trade strata, and repeated threshold selection against the sealed holdout would overfit it. The proposal also omits validation that abstention does not concentrate exposure or degrade portfolio-level risk controls.
