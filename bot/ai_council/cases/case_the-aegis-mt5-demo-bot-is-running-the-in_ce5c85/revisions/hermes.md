Revised proposal: make the validated-state gate EV-net-of-costs aware using strictly decision-time information — net EV computed from contemporaneous executable quotes captured at signal time, charged through a slippage model frozen and version-pinned before any evaluation, with the EV horizon and cost model fixed in a written pre-registration before any holdout contact. All rule construction and threshold selection (including any refinement of the "skip when net EV <= 0" predicate) occurs only on an earlier development window under predefined effect-size and minimum-sample-per-stratum criteria; the sealed holdout is then evaluated exactly once with block-bootstrap confidence intervals that respect temporal dependence and a multiplicity correction over the small pre-declared variant set, so repeated testing against the holdout is structurally impossible. Acceptance additionally requires the uplift to replicate out-of-sample when stratified by symbol, session, and regime (with sparse strata pooled by prespecified rules) and requires demonstrated non-degradation of exposure concentration and all portfolio-level risk controls. The abstention rule acts purely as an additional entry-suppression predicate inside the existing champion gate — it adds no order path, touches no broker interface, and cannot alter governed DEMO execution, which remains solely owned by run_broker_paper.py. If the uplift fails to replicate or any robustness check fails, the null result is recorded honestly in the ExperimentRegistry and the champion is left untouched.
