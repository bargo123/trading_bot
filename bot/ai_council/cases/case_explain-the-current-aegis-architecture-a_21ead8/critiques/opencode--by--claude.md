Running all 25+ catalogue algos against the same EURUSD dataset and promoting whichever clears "OOS expectancy > 0 and PF > 1" is textbook multiple-comparison mining — with ~25 trials you should expect a winner by chance, and the proposal specifies no family-wise correction, no deflated-Sharpe adjustment, and no rule preventing the sealed holdout from being consumed 25 times, which un-seals it on first use. The `wins_erased_by_average_loss < 4` gate is an unjustified free parameter, and at the base 1:30 payoff geometry it is arithmetically unreachable without changing exits, so the criterion is either dead on arrival or quietly implies the algos are being tested on a different exit structure than the CORE baseline — which also breaks the M1-vs-M5/M15 comparison you claim to be making. The premise that a meta-filter can rescue PF 0.27 is unmeasured hand-waving: at a 1-pip TP the entire edge lives inside spread, commission, slippage, and fill latency, and nothing here states those are modeled, so any positive OOS expectancy is unfalsifiable rather than real. Before this is worth council time, specify the cost model, pre-register a single holdout touch with a corrected significance threshold, and justify the 4-wins threshold from the payoff distribution instead of asserting it.
