Your MFE join is structurally impossible: `reports/firehose_mfe.json` is a per-symbol live snapshot (`dict[str, float]`) with no timestamps, no ticket IDs, and no history — keys are popped on close (`exits.py:205-224`, runner `:1259-1260`) — so there is nothing to join against journal timestamps. You acknowledge MAE needs backfilling but fail to notice MFE needs an entirely new per-ticket instrumentation layer written at close time; this isn't a missing file, it's a missing data architecture. Additionally, your framing of the `manual` bucket is wrong: `close_reason()` (`reconcile.py:234-235`) classifies every `aegis_flatten` comment as `"manual"`, which bundles at least five distinct runtime sub-exits (giveback, quick_win, never_green, max_hold, session flatten) — conflating them into one cohort before measuring MFE-giveback will dilute any signal, since a quick_win flatten that caught a 2-pip overshoot and a max_hold flatten that bled from +1R to -0.5R have opposite implications for your hypothesis. Finally, your "measurement only" framing is misleading when the measurement itself requires new code to emit per-ticket MFE at flatten time, write it to a durable log, and backfill 1891 historical exits — that is a feature build, not an observation, and should be costed as such in the council proposal.
