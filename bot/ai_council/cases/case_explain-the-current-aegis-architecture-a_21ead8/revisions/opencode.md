AEGIS is a five-layer quant research system — ingestion (MT5 read-only), a 71-module offline research layer (25+ algo catalogue, 40+ intel meta-filters, analogue store, thesis fire/sizing/economics), governed champion promotion with bootstrap stress and sealed holdouts, a paper-only runner, and an optimizer cycle — all anchored to a net-negative base strategy (EURUSD M1, 1-pip TP / 30-pip SL, 95.7% win rate, PF 0.27) that the intelligent layer is designed to filter into profitability; the cost model explicitly incorporates spread (1.2 pips), commission ($7 RT), slippage (0.3 pips), and fill-latency delay (200 ms) into all backtested expectancy figures so that no positive OOS result can survive on unfalsifiable assumptions. No challenger has yet cleared the full promotion gauntlet; `intelligent_champion.json` remains unpopulated. The most promising untested lever is the 25+ algorithm catalogue in `session_algos.py` (Hale, Volman, Chan, Fabris, Steidl, etc.), which is registered but has never been systematically evaluated through the governed walk-forward → sealed-holdout → bootstrap promotion path that GEN1–GEN6 apply to the CORE firehose meta-filters; to guard against multiple-comparison mining, the experiment pre-registers a single holdout touch with a Bonferroni-corrected α (0.05/25 = 0.002) and a deflated-Sharpe adjustment for the 25 simultaneous trials, and the sealed holdout is consumed once for the family, not per-algo. The `wins_erased_by_average_loss` gate is replaced with a threshold derived directly from the empirical payoff distribution of each candidate algo — specifically, the maximum number of consecutive average-sized losses that the algo's OOS profit factor can absorb before PF drops below 1 — eliminating the prior arbitrary "< 4" criterion and making the exit structure transparently comparable to the CORE baseline on the same M5/M15 dataset. If any single algo, after family-wise correction, achieves net-of-cost OOS expectancy > 0 with PF > 1 and survives a single pre-registered holdout test, it becomes the first candidate worthy of governed promotion review.
