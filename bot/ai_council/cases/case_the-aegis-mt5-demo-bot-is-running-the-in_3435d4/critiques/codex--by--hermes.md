The core flaw is that it mines an already-sealed holdout: replaying "the same" sealed set with and without a variant turns the 15% scratch-reduction threshold into post-hoc selection on test data, and with typically small invalidated/scratch counts that delta can easily be noise — it needs a pre-registered hypothesis on a development split, then a genuinely untouched confirmation window, with bootstrap CIs or significance testing rather than a raw percentage gate. Its key terms are unmeasurable as written: "quote freshness," "spread stability," and "tail loss" have no numeric definitions, tolerances, or sampling specification, so the adoption gate cannot be objectively evaluated or audited. It also silently assumes deterministic replay, but signal-to-order persistence changes fill timing, so the two arms experience different realized spreads and the comparison isn't clean unless both arms are re-priced under identical cost assumptions. Finally, it omits mandatory governance — no ExperimentRegistry registration and no promotion through `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` gates, which "adopt it" would require even though the DEMO-only, net-of-costs framing itself respects the safety envelope.
