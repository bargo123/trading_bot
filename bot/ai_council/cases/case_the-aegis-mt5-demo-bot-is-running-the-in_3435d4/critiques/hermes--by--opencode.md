The “roughly half” threshold is arbitrary and does not establish whether the gate improves future performance rather than simply rejects low-margin strategies. Recent Firehose spreads are regime- and symbol-dependent; applying a pooled 90th-percentile cost can misprice historical trades unless stress is computed per instrument, session, and execution context. Re-scoring stored candidates is retrospective selection analysis, not validation: the gate needs an untouched forward or sealed holdout test and explicit handling for incomplete per-trade economics.
