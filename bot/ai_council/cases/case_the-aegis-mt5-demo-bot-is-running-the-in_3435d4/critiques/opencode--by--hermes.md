The proposal never specifies the candidate threshold value or how it was chosen — if multiple thresholds are swept and the best performer is picked using the sealed holdout, that holdout becomes a selection surface and the design is burned by multiple-comparison overfitting; a single pre-registered value selected on train-period data alone must be pinned in the registry before any holdout touch. "Higher net expectancy" carries no minimum effect size or significance requirement, so a difference well inside bootstrap noise would pass, and raising the sample floor mechanically shrinks trade count, inflating per-trade expectancy while reducing aggregate edge and statistical power — the comparison should also require non-inferior total net PnL or exposure-matched outcomes, not just per-trade expectancy. Maximum-drawdown comparison is apples-to-oranges unless both runs share identical windows, symbols, and executable-overlap accounting, and a drawdown tie at tiny sample sizes is indistinguishable from luck. Safety-wise the read-only, gated framing is compliant, but the registry entry must fix the hypothesis, exact threshold, and acceptance criteria in advance, otherwise this is an unblinded fishing expedition dressed as validation.
