Proposal: add a pre-registered cost-stress robustness gate to the promotion path: a challenger passes only if its primary split-OOS expectancy remains non-negative when each recorded per-trade cost is inflated by a single fixed multiplier (1.5x that trade's own logged spread/slippage), computed per instrument and session from the experiment registry's existing per-trade economics, with trades lacking decomposed costs conservatively re-scored at the worst observed cost for that symbol-session or the challenger fails the gate and the incompleteness is logged. Validation is prospective, not retrospective: the rule applies only to challengers entering promotion after its registration date, sealed holdouts and already-stored candidates are never rescored, and the gate's predictive validity is tested by whether stressed-cost expectancy rank-correlates with realized sealed-holdout/forward expectancy across the first N=20 post-adoption challengers (predeclared Spearman rho > 0 with p < 0.05), rather than by any pass-rate threshold. Separately and only if governance explicitly authorizes it, a predeclared champion-fill audit (next 200 DEMO fills or 60 days, whichever first; one-sided test that realized fills' spread distribution exceeds the 75th percentile of logged spreads less than 5% of the time) checks whether live execution costs actually concentrate near historical stress levels. No changes to champion logic, no MT5 interaction, no new data collection beyond what the registry and outcome logs already record.
