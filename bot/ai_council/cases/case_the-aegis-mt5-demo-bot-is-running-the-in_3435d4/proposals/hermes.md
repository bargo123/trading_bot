Proposal: add a cost-stress robustness gate to the validated-state promotion path — a challenger may pass only if its primary split-OOS expectancy stays non-negative when evaluated at stressed costs (e.g., 1.5x its configured spread/slippage assumption, or the 90th percentile of recently logged Firehose spreads), computed from the already-recorded per-trade economics in the experiment registry rather than any new data collection. This is directly falsifiable: re-score every challenger currently stored in bot/research/strategies/ and sealed_holdouts.jsonl under the stressed-cost rule, and register the outcome in ExperimentRegistry; the hypothesis fails if more than roughly half of current gate-passers remain profitable under stress, which would show mean-cost assumptions are not the binding constraint and the gate adds no selectivity. Conversely, if most current passers flip negative under stress while the champion's live DEMO fills cluster near the 90th-percentile spread, the gate identifies real survivorship from optimistic fill assumptions. This extends existing rule that all outcomes be net of runner spread/slippage and requires no code changes to the champion, no MT5 interaction, and no new corpus assumptions (no matched passages).
