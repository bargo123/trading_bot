The “independent” analogue/outcome model may share data, features, or tuning decisions with the validated-state gate, making agreement a correlated filter rather than genuine confirmation and inviting overfitting. “Calibration” and the 10% expectancy threshold lack predefined statistical tests, confidence bounds, sample-size requirements, and multiple-comparison controls. A single next holdout is insufficient to establish robustness across regimes; require frozen specifications and additional temporal/regime holdouts before any governed promotion.
