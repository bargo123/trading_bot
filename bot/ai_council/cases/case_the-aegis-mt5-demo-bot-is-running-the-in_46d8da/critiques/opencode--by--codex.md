The proposal risks overfitting by tuning and judging a new rule directly on the sealed holdout, which ceases to be a valid holdout if evaluated repeatedly. “Higher profit factor” and “no increase in maximum drawdown” are unmeasurable without minimum trade counts, confidence intervals, test horizons, and precise ATR/percentile definitions; PF is especially unstable with sparse trades. It also omits walk-forward and regime-specific validation, cost sensitivity, and mandatory experiment-registry/governance checks before any promotion.
