Revised proposal: The failure audit shows all 470 OMS rejects were `stops` while true-TP exits earned +0.09 expectancy versus −0.017 on the manual/flatten path, motivating a demo-only diagnostic-and-counterfactual experiment (registered in `ExperimentRegistry`) testing whether SL placement violates the broker min-stop distance. Instead of probing reject responses, we will read each symbol's minimum stop level directly from MT5's read-only symbol properties (`SYMBOL_TRADE_STOPS_LEVEL` plus spread buffer) and verify against any documented broker specs — no order submission of any kind. Because widened stops change intratrade paths, the counterfactual will be replayed bar-by-bar over an extended window using stored M1 price paths with the runner's existing spread/slippage assumptions applied to every simulated fill, and with the flatten policy (`quick_win`/`gave_back`) re-evaluated against the wider stops rather than assumed unchanged. To guard against post-hoc overfitting, the buffer parameter will be tuned only on an in-sample segment, then frozen and evaluated on a pre-registered sealed out-of-sample holdout. Success is falsifiable on two measurable metrics: (1) forward demo-window `oms_reject{reason=stops}` count equal to 0 across at least N≥200 placed stops, and (2) holdout counterfactual net exceeding realized net with a cluster bootstrap CI (resampling by day×symbol clusters) excluding zero; if either fails, the hypothesis is rejected and recorded as such. No champion change, no live YAML touch, and the validated-state gate remains until standard promotion gates pass.
