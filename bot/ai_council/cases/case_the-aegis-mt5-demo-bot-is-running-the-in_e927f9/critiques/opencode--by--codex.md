MT5 reject responses do not reliably reveal each symbol’s exact minimum stop distance, so the proposed measurement method is underspecified and may require prohibited order probing rather than read-only access. A seven-day, post-hoc replay on the same failures that generated the hypothesis invites severe overfitting, and widened stops cannot be counterfactually scored from closed-trade outcomes without intratrade price paths, fill/slippage modeling, and explicit flatten-policy interactions. “Rejects ≈ 0” is unmeasurable as written, while a bootstrap CI over correlated trades is invalid unless clustered by time/symbol and confirmed on a sealed out-of-sample holdout.
