Tuning the confidence threshold by scoring "all threshold variants" against the *same sealed holdouts* used for the accept/reject decision converts those holdouts into a selection set—this is textbook multiple-testing overfitting, and reusing an already-burned seal for the champion's challenger compounds the contamination; there is no held-out confirmation pass after threshold selection. The 5% net-expectancy bar carries no statistical significance requirement (bootstrap or otherwise), no statement of whether it's relative to champion expectancy on identical windows, and ignores that DEMO fills make the cost assumptions optimistic rather than evidence-based. The trade-count (−20%) and drawdown tolerances are arbitrary round numbers with no pre-registered rationale, and "worsening maximum drawdown" lacks any epsilon, making the gate either trivially strict or silently discretionary. Finally, "stability gate fails → reject" is asserted without defining the stability metric, window, or false-positive handling—an unmeasurable claim dressed as a safeguard.
