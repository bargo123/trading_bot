The fired-versus-skipped comparison is selection-confounded: the gate determines exposure, so skipped states may lack comparable realized exits, and fixed-horizon synthetic outcomes are not equivalent to strategy P&L. Testing many symbols and 1–10-bar horizons without a pre-registered primary endpoint, multiplicity correction, purged time splits, and a sealed holdout invites overfitting; an ordinary bootstrap also ignores serial dependence. Re-mining “positive pockets” from already inspected outcomes compounds selection bias, and “CI excluding zero” alone omits minimum effect size, sample-size, regime-stability, and drawdown gates.
