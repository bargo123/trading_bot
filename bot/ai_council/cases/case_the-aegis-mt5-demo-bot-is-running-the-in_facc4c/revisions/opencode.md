**Proposal (rev 2): Gate-validity audit — pre-registered, purged replay of fired-vs-skipped states.** Because the gate itself determines exposure, the replay scores both arms under one identical synthetic forward-return metric (explicitly a gate-quality diagnostic, not a P&L forecast): the pre-registered primary endpoint is pooled fired-minus-matched-skipped net-of-cost expectancy at the 5-bar horizon, where every fired state is matched to nearest-neighbour skipped states on symbol/session/volatility/spread covariates, with 1–10 bar horizons demoted to a sensitivity family under Holm correction. Inference uses a moving-block bootstrap sized to one trading day to respect serial dependence and passes only if the 95% CI excludes zero AND the difference clears a pre-registered minimum effect size (≥0.05R/trade) at n≥200 per arm, with additional sign-consistency-across-quarters and max-drawdown gates. Time splits are purged and embargoed: candidate validated states are re-derived from scratch on the training window alone — outcome_learning.md's USDCHF/USDCAD pockets are treated strictly as hypothesis-generating, never as confirmatory evidence — and accepted only if they replicate on a fresh sealed holdout registered in advance. Endpoints, thresholds, split boundaries, and holdout seals are frozen in ExperimentRegistry before any replay executes; a holdout failure declares the validated set mis-specified and triggers governed re-mining, not adjustment of this experiment. Research-only throughout: no code changes, no champion promotion, no live config touched.
