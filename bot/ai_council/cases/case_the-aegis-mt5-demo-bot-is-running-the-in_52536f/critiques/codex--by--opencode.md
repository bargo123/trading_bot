“Two consecutive scans” is undefined without scan cadence, state-transition rules, and a pre-specified threshold for “material” coverage loss, making the outcome easy to tune post hoc. A single sealed-holdout comparison risks overfitting to timing and regime composition; require walk-forward or multiple untouched holdouts with confidence intervals and net results under stressed spread/slippage.
