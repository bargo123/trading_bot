A single sealed holdout comparison is vulnerable to noise and overfitting, while the arbitrary 0.05 threshold shift and 10% expectancy uplift lack statistical or economic justification. “No increase” in maximum drawdown is an unstable path-dependent criterion, and allowing a 25% trade-count reduction can improve expectancy by discarding marginal trades without demonstrating superior portfolio risk-adjusted performance. The proposal also omits preregistration in the ExperimentRegistry, confidence intervals/significance testing, multiple-period or regime validation, and explicit confirmation that all spread, slippage, and execution assumptions are conservative and leak-free.
