The evidence is observational and confounded: TP closures are selected by trades that reach TP, while manual-flatten counts are internally inconsistent (1,383 + 1,288 + 229 exceeds 1,928 unless categories overlap), so it does not establish that early exits cause the losses. “Validated states” and the ≥+0.01/−0.55 thresholds appear outcome-tuned, while sealed-holdout counterfactual replay cannot reliably model fills, spread/slippage, duration, concurrency, or exposure changes from waiting for barriers. The implementation must also prove it never alters protected live YAML or enables live trading, and require walk-forward, regime/symbol stratification, and independently defined tail and cost metrics before any promotion.
