Revised proposal: Add a hard tail-loss exit challenger, but replace single-point tuning with a pre-registered sensitivity sweep over stop caps (0.15–0.45 in 0.05 steps) selected by walk-forward across distinct regime windows, choosing the cap on training folds only so no level is fit to evaluation data; every sweep and tuning run is logged in the ExperimentRegistry before execution. Because recorded `outcome_log` exits cannot establish intratrade stop behavior, the replay reconstructs each trade from stored M1 price paths, filling synthetic stops conservatively (worst of stop level vs. next-bar open, plus a fixed slippage/gap buffer), and separately reports the counterfactual recovery rate of would-be-stopped trades instead of assuming it. Acceptance gates, declared in advance: on a sealed holdout untouched by tuning, spanning ≥2 regimes and meeting a minimum sample of ~300 qualifying exits, the challenger must show PF > 1.0 with a 95% bootstrap CI excluding 1.0, wins_erased_by_tail_loss < 3, win rate ≥ 55% (CI non-inferior to baseline), all net of runner spread/slippage under both base and stressed cost assumptions. Additionally, Monte Carlo resampling of holdout trades must demonstrate lower tail-drawdown and risk-of-ruin than the unmanaged baseline, with all uncertainty bounds reported alongside point estimates. If any gate fails—or if intrabar data proves insufficient to verify fill assumptions—the challenger is rejected and recorded as failed in the registry; no champion or live config is modified, consistent with Davey's sealed-validation and Monte Carlo discipline.
