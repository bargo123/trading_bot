The 0.30 cap is tuned from the same observed loss distribution it aims to improve, so a single sealed holdout is insufficient without walk-forward testing, untouched multi-regime data, and sensitivity analysis across stop levels. A replay based only on recorded exits cannot reliably model intratrade stop triggering, gaps, slippage, or whether stopped trades would later recover, making the PF and win-rate predictions potentially unmeasurable from `outcome_log` alone. The proposal also omits uncertainty bounds, sample-size requirements, Monte Carlo results, and explicit proof that every experiment—including tuning runs—is registered.
