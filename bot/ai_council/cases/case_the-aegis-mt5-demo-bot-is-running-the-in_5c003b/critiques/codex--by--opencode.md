Replaying on the *same* sealed DEMO dataset after each iteration is the fatal flaw: every accept/reject decision spent against a reused holdout turns it into a selection surface, so reported gains become a multiple-testing artifact rather than evidence of generalization — the seal exists precisely so it can be spent once. The acceptance rule is under-specified and statistically weak: no minimum effect size or significance test (e.g., bootstrap on net expectancy/DD), an unjustified magic cutoff of ATR>75, no confirmation that model confidence is calibrated across volatility regimes, and no guarantee the ATR percentile itself uses only closed bars (lookahead risk) — all compounded by a small high-volatility subsample that 80% trade-count retention will shrink further toward noise. Defining bespoke accept criteria also sidesteps governance: the candidate must still clear every gate in `bot/aegis/research/promote.py`, not merely this proposal's two-metric comparison, and a rejected challenger must be recorded in `bot/research/strategies/` and the sealed-holdout ledger, which the proposal omits.
