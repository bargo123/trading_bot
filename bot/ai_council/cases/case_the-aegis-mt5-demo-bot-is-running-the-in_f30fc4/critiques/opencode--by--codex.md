The 90th-percentile/60-day/70%-trade-count thresholds are arbitrary hyperparameters with substantial overfitting risk, especially if chosen after inspecting results. “Net expectancy improves” and “drawdown does not worsen” lack confidence intervals, statistical tests, and a defined comparison horizon, while a single sealed holdout cannot establish robustness across regimes. The proposal also omits mandatory ExperimentRegistry recording and explicit confirmation that implementation remains isolated from immutable live configuration and promotion gates.
