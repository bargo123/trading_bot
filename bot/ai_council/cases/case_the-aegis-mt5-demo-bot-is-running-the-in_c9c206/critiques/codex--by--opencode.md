This proposal replays a holdout already consumed by the incumbent champion, so any challenger tuned to pass it is doing textbook selection bias on a spent dataset — there's no fresh out-of-sample segment, walk-forward, or multiplicity correction despite presumably many such challengers. The acceptance criteria are statistically hollow: a 10% *relative* expectancy gain on as few as ~30 trades is noise without confidence intervals or bootstrap, and "drawdown does not worsen" is confounded since a higher threshold mechanically trades less, flattering DD while gutting sample size. "Positive net expected value after measured spread and slippage" is unmeasurable as written — measured over which regime, and applied point-in-time or with hindsight (lookahead risk)? Finally, it never commits to registering the experiment in the ExperimentRegistry *before* execution or to routing acceptance through every gate in `bot/aegis/research/promote.py`, both of which are non-negotiable.
