The causal claim that “loss geometry, not signal quality” is unsupported: a high win rate with poor payoff can still arise from weak or regime-dependent entries, and counterfactual exits cannot recover unobserved intrabar execution from a reconciled close log. Testing multiple stop/time-stop variants on the same 2,517 trades invites selection overfitting unless variants are preregistered, walk-forward folds are temporally purged, and the ≥200-trade holdout is untouched until final evaluation with uncertainty bounds and regime stability checks. The ~55% win-rate floor and book-memory note count are arbitrary, weakly measurable justifications; the real gate should include statistically credible net expectancy/PF, drawdown and tail-loss constraints, plus explicit confirmation that spread, slippage, gaps, and stop-fill mechanics are modeled without lookahead.
