The similarity threshold is a tunable selection parameter, so optimizing it against one sealed holdout risks overfitting and invalidates that holdout; require nested or rolling out-of-sample validation with an untouched final set. “Identical DEMO costs” may not capture live spread, slippage, latency, and regime changes, while net expectancy and maximum drawdown lack confidence intervals and statistical significance requirements. The proposal also omits minimum sample/trade-count and robustness gates, and changing the validated-state gate could violate the prohibition on modifying live runtime configuration unless implemented strictly as a research-only artifact.
