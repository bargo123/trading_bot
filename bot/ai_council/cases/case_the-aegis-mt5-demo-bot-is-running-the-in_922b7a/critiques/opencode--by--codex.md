“Improves expectancy” and “does not worsen maximum drawdown” lack predefined effect sizes, confidence thresholds, and statistical tests, making acceptance subjective and vulnerable to cherry-picking. The rolling median and ATR window are unspecified and could introduce lookahead or overfit tuning; a single sealed holdout also cannot establish robustness across regimes or repeated research iterations. The proposal omits minimum trade counts, walk-forward/temporal validation, and controls for multiple comparisons, while assuming configured spread/slippage accurately represents execution costs.
