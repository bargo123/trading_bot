The evaluation design leaks: iterating filter variants against "sealed" holdouts converts the seal into a de facto training set after the first rejection — there's no untouched intermediate validation tier, no pre-registered single-shot protocol, and no multiple-comparison control if thresholds get swept. The acceptance rule is statistically hollow: no minimum trade count or significance requirement on net expectancy means noise passes, and comparing raw maximum drawdown between the champion and a stricter variant with very different trade counts is apples-to-oranges absent normalization or aligned periods. The 2:1 remaining reward-to-risk floor is asserted without justification or sensitivity analysis, and "positive expected value" lacks a stated horizon, sample window, and cost basis beyond an unspecified spread/slippage assumption. Trade count is measured yet excluded from the decision, so a three-trade fluke could be promoted — any run belongs in the ExperimentRegistry with promotion only through the governed `promote.py` gates, and this memo authorizes nothing.
