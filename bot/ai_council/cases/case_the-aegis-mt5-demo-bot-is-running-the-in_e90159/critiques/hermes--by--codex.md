The audit is selection-biased: rejected states are precisely those with stale, invalid, or incomplete inputs, so replaying them through a champion that requires valid state may invent fills, prices, and exits rather than measure executable counterfactuals. “95% CI spanning zero” does not establish that the gate costs little edge; the arbitrary ≥200 sample target, session dependence, multiple testing, and undefined bootstrap unit/effect size leave the claim underpowered and unmeasurable. A relaxation is also not automatically authorized by `promote.py`: changing a validated-state gate requires separate safety review, protected-config governance, and proof that the replayed alpha survives real-time quote, spread, OMS, and fail-closed constraints.
