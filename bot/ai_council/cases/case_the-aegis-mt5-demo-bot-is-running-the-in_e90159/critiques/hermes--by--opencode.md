Offline replay cannot establish counterfactual fills or exits from stale/unvalidated states; quoted spread/slippage models may be systematically optimistic exactly where the gate rejects. “≥200 rejections” does not ensure independent samples, session/symbol balance, or sufficient power, while comparing rejected simulations to admitted realized trades confounds state quality with market regime and selection bias. A mean-EV test also hides tail losses, execution failures, and correlated drawdowns, so threshold correctness is not demonstrated.
