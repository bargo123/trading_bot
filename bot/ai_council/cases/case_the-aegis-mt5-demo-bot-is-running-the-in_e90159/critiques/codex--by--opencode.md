The “validated-state” definition is unspecified, inviting threshold tuning on the same replay used for selection. Adverse excursion, net expectancy, trade-count adjustment, and abstention limits need fixed formulas, confidence intervals, and a sealed out-of-sample period; otherwise “improvement” is unmeasurable. Leaving sizing unchanged does not establish safety if the higher gate shifts exposure into correlated session regimes or if DEMO spread/slippage assumptions understate executable costs.
