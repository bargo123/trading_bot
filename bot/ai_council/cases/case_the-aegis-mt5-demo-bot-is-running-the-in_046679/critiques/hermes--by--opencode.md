The 75th-percentile threshold and 24-hour window appear tuned from the same autopsy evidence, with no multiple-testing control or pre-registered symbol/session stratification. A two-week filter-on/filter-off comparison is vulnerable to time, regime, and opportunity-set confounding unless trades are randomly assigned concurrently; it also cannot claim the filter caused changes in loss count. Scoring against assumed costs contradicts the stated problem of realized spreads exceeding assumptions: evaluate actual quoted and filled costs, with minimum sample sizes and confidence intervals specified before the test.
