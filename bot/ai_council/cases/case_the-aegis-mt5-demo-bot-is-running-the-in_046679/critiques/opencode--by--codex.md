The 25% threshold and 80% trade-count floor are arbitrary and invite overfitting, especially if this sealed holdout is consulted repeatedly during tuning. “Net expectancy improves” is incomplete without confidence intervals, regime/session breakdowns, statistical tests, and sensitivity to spread/slippage estimation; maximum drawdown alone is an unstable acceptance criterion. The rolling median must be strictly point-in-time and validated under realistic quote gaps, illiquidity, and execution latency, or the gate may introduce lookahead and fail operationally.
