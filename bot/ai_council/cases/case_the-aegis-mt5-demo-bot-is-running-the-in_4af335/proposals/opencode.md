Proposal: Run a point-in-time counterfactual exit-policy study as the next cycle's headline experiment. Measured evidence shows aggregate demo performance of ~2,471 trades at WR 67.8% but PF 0.77 / expectancy −0.011 (net ≈ −27 USD), with the entire loss concentrated in `manual` closes (n=1,917, PF 0.60, net −31) and `sl` closes (n=94, expectancy −0.55), while `tp` closes are the only positive bucket (n=446, expectancy +0.106) — a payoff-geometry failure (avg win 0.058 vs avg loss −0.170), not an entry-selection failure. Since every close already records MFE/MAE/giveback and per-policy counterfactual profits (`cf_*` fields), replay the same ~2,400-trade sample under one mechanical alternative — hold-to-TP with a cost-plus breakeven lock once MFE ≥ $0.30 and giveback ≥ 50% (parameters already config-driven in `profit_management.py`) — and pre-register the falsifiable claim that this flips aggregate net expectancy from −0.011 to ≥ +0.01 after measured per-symbol/session costs. If the counterfactual fails, or the improvement does not survive purged/time-aware out-of-sample evaluation, register the rejection in `ExperimentRegistry` and block rediscovery; if it passes all gates in `promote.py`, route it solely as a challenger via the governed promotion path (the profit-management layer must not weaken champion gates). This follows the test-everything discipline of *Evidence-Based Technical Analysis* (Aronson) and the let-winners-run/mechanical-exit principles in *Following the Trend* (Wiley, 2023).
