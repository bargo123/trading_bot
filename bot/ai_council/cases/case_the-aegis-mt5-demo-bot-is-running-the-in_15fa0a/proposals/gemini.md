I propose a research cycle focused on 'Dynamic Validation Sensitivity' where the validated-state gate's confidence threshold is scaled by the 1-minute ATR to account for execution lag in high-volatility environments. Drawing from the regime-filtering concepts in *Trading Price Action Trends*, this candidate assumes that slippage in the MT5 DEMO environment currently invalidates states that appear profitable in backtests. We will test this by requiring a 20% higher validation score when M1 volatility is in the top decile. This proposal is falsifiable: it will be considered a failure if it does not increase the demo Win Rate by at least 5% over a 100-trade sample compared to the current static gate.
