The top-decile cutoff and 20% threshold increase appear arbitrarily tuned, creating substantial overfitting risk unless preregistered and tested on sealed out-of-sample data across multiple regimes. A 100-trade demo sample is likely underpowered, and “5% higher win rate” lacks confidence intervals, net profitability, drawdown, and spread/slippage controls; win rate alone can improve while expectancy worsens. The proposal also assumes slippage causes validation failures without measuring latency or realized slippage, and it omits ExperimentRegistry recording, lookahead-safe ATR construction, baseline tests, and governed promotion gates.
