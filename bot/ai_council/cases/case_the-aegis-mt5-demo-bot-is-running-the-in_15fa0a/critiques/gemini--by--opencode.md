The 100-trade win-rate criterion is statistically vacuous — at n≈100 the sampling noise alone (~5pp standard error) swamps the claimed 5% effect, win rate ignores payoff asymmetry so the gate can "pass" while net expectancy degrades, and no sealed holdout or walk-forward split means the 20% bump and top-decile ATR cutoff are just two more tunable magic numbers inviting overfit with zero multiple-comparison control. The central premise — that demo slippage invalidates backtest-profitable states — is asserted, never measured; there is no plan to log actual slippage-vs-ATR distributions against the runner's cost assumptions before building a gate around them. Raising the threshold mechanically suppresses trade count, so the pass/fail test conflates selection effects with improvement, risks never reaching n=100 in any useful window, and cites a trading book as if it were evidence rather than decoration. Finally, implementation must stay confined to `bot/research/` configs and the experiment registry — any change to the runtime validated-state gate or champion logic without the full promotion gates is a governance violation, and this proposal doesn't say where its changes live.
