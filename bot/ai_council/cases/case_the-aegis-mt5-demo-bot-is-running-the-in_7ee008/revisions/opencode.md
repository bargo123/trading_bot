Revised proposal: Build a research-only counterfactual shadow ledger that replays every `state_not_in_validated_set` skip strictly point-in-time (features computed only from bars available at each decision timestamp) through a champion whose parameters and validated-state set are frozen as-of a pre-declared cut date, with all cluster discovery done in-sample and the post-cut window sealed as an untouched holdout in `bot/research/sealed_holdouts.jsonl`. To control the ~dozens of simultaneous cluster tests (symbol × session × side), require in-sample stationary-block-bootstrap p05 net expectancy > 0 with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR ≤ 0.10 across clusters, a minimum of 500 shadow exits per cluster after enforcing ≥ one-session spacing between entries (reporting effective sample size, since raw counts overstate independence), and replication on the sealed holdout where the cluster's point estimate must exceed 0 with a 90% bootstrap interval excluding zero. Costs use the runner's 0.3-pip assumption only as a floor: fills are additionally priced from empirical demo spread distributions at the original skip timestamps (capturing session-dependent widening and adverse-selection timing) and stressed at 2× and 3×, with adoption requiring positive expectancy under the median stress case. "Validated states retain their edge" is operationalized as their in-sample net expectancy falling within the 90% bootstrap interval of their trailing-window realized expectancy; if the validated states themselves degrade, the experiment is recorded as voided rather than read as evidence either way. The skip label itself is treated as a hypothesis, not proof of gate correctness: both outcomes — narrow-gate confirmation or a challenger cluster — are pre-registered in the ExperimentRegistry before any replay runs, any surviving challenger proceeds through the full `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` gate stack (never direct gate edits), and rejections are logged honestly with full metrics. This requires no live orders, no `allow_live` change, and no champion edit.
