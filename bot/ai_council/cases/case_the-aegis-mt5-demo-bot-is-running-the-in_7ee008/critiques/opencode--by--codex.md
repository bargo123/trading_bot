The proposal risks severe selection and look-ahead bias: replaying skipped states through the champion’s exit policy on demo bars, then selecting clusters that pass a bootstrap p05 threshold, reuses the champion and searches many clusters without an untouched holdout or multiple-testing correction. “Validated states retain their edge” and “positive bootstrap-p05” are underspecified without a fixed time split, minimum trade independence, confidence procedure, and exact cost/slippage model; 500 shadow exits can still be highly correlated and nonrepresentative. It also treats the observed skip label as proof the narrow gate is correct, while the reported expectancy and 0.3-pip assumption are not independently validated against realistic fills, regime drift, or adverse selection.
