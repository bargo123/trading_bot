The fixed abstention margin is a free parameter, and neither its value nor the tolerances defining "comparable coverage" or "worsening drawdown" are pre-registered, making the falsification clause unfalsifiable in practice and the whole comparison a garden of forking paths. Evaluating on sealed holdouts "in the next walk-forward cycle" is ambiguous between repeated peeking (which destroys their sealed status) and a single underpowered draw with no stated bootstrap CI, significance test, or minimum trade count for expectancy deltas that are noise-dominated after costs. Nothing specifies how the spread/slippage buffer is estimated—static runner assumptions versus realized, session-dependent costs—so the gate may simply memorize one cost regime and fail out-of-sample. It also omits the mandatory ExperimentRegistry entry and the governed promotion path (`promote.py` gates via `research_promote_champion.py`), leaving undefined what happens if the challenger "passes."
