The 1.5× spread/slippage rule is underspecified and potentially circular because “predicted net expectancy” already depends on cost estimates whose calibration and uncertainty are not defined. A single sealed period with no pre-registered data boundaries, walk-forward validation, significance/confidence criteria, or lookahead audit cannot establish robustness; the trade-count and max-drawdown gates also permit noisy, economically insignificant improvements and unstable profit-factor comparisons. The proposal omits mandatory ExperimentRegistry recording and explicit confirmation that DEMO eligibility cannot reach live execution or modify protected champion/live configuration.
