The 90th-percentile ATR cutoff and the +0.05R acceptance threshold are arbitrary hyperparameters that invite overfitting, especially if selected after inspecting holdout results. “No drawdown increase” is unmeasurable without a defined estimator, confidence bounds, and tolerance for sampling noise, while a single sealed holdout provides no evidence of stability across instruments, periods, or volatility regimes. The proposal also omits explicit registry recording, runner cost assumptions, minimum sample-size/power requirements, and confirmation that ATR is computed strictly without lookahead.
