The proposal never specifies how many challengers have already been tested against this same "sealed" holdout — repeated head-to-heads on one fixed window inflate the winner's curse, so an improvement claim is meaningless without a pre-registered effect threshold, confidence intervals, and correction for the family of prior trials. "Improves expectancy and drawdown" and "no increase in false-positive entry rate" are unmeasurable as written: there is no defined ground truth for a "false positive," no minimum trade count for significance, and single-path drawdown on a short holdout is noise, not evidence. It also omits the leakage check that matters most — a short-horizon volatility-regime feature must be computed strictly point-in-time at decision time, and nothing in the proposal states how that will be verified before the run. Finally, "promote it only if…" reads as self-certification against ad-hoc criteria; promotion is lawful only through `bot/aegis/research/promote.py`'s full gate suite via the sanctioned scripts, and the experiment must be logged in `ExperimentRegistry` — neither is mentioned, so reject pending a rewritten protocol.
