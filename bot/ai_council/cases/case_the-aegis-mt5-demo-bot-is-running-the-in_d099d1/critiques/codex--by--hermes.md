The proposal's five conjunctive cutoffs (≤2s, three polls, ≥10% expectancy, ≥15% excursion, ≤25% trade loss) are unprovenanced magic numbers with no pre-registration in the ExperimentRegistry before holdout contact, creating classic threshold-shopping overfitting risk, and "admissible spread" is left undefined so the gate can be tightened or loosened post hoc until the accept conditions trip. The claims aren't measurable as written: no baseline window or confidence interval backs the 10%/15% improvements against single-holdout noise (no significance test or multiple-comparison guard despite five simultaneous gates), "adverse first-minute excursion" is undefined, and "freshness ≤2s for three consecutive polls" is self-contradictory unless poll cadence is specified. Most critically, the validation design is confounded: the gate changes which trades exist, so expectancy and excursion must be compared on a paired/matched opportunity set with counterfactuals for blocked entries, not raw aggregates over two different trade populations, and there is no walk-forward replication requirement before a single-holdout pass promotes a challenger. Safety posture is otherwise compliant (DEMO-only, governed promote/reject path), but absent these fixes the acceptance rule would certify noise.
