The proposal is vulnerable to overfitting because it does not pre-specify the volatility definition, lookback, thresholds, tuning procedure, or multiple-testing correction; a single sealed holdout is insufficient evidence. “Improves” expectancy, drawdown, and false-positive rate are unmeasurable without confidence intervals, minimum sample/trade counts, regime-stratified validation, and explicit metric definitions. It also omits registry recording and governed promotion checks, and “without increasing trade risk” is not a demonstrated safety control unless enforced by the existing risk and execution gates.
