The proposal leaves its central hyperparameter—the cost-share threshold—undefined in selection methodology; tune it on the same walk-forward folds used for evaluation and you've just fit noise with extra steps, so it demands nested selection or a frozen a priori value. "Improves held-out net expectancy" and "worsening calibration" are unmeasurable as written: no minimum effect size, no statistical significance or bootstrap interval, and no named calibration metric or numeric drawdown gate—predefined gates that aren't stated can't be audited post hoc. Validation gaps include no regime/session stratification check (a cost filter plausibly helps only in thin-liquidity sessions), no multi-split robustness, and no mention of registering the experiment in `ExperimentRegistry` or sealing the holdout before any tuning occurs, all mandated by council rules. Finally, since this challenger alters live entry behavior upon promotion, the proposal must explicitly route through the governed `promote.py` gates and touch no protected live YAML—silence on that is a safety violation waiting to happen.
