Daily rebuilding invites repeated-testing and regime-overfitting unless states are evaluated on untouched, time-forward holdouts with multiplicity correction and version-frozen selection rules. The forward criterion is underspecified: “materially negative,” the observation window, confidence threshold, and treatment of overlapping/correlated fires are undefined, while ≥50 fires with expectancy merely ≥0 is too weak to establish robustness. Sealing only after failure does not prevent repeatedly adapting to the same demo stream, and the proposal omits rollback/quarantine rules if an artifact consumed by runtime logic admits a harmful state.
