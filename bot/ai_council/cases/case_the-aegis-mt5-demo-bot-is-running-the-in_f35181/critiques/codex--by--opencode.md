The "every market regime" requirement is unmeasurable as written—regimes are never defined (count, labeling method, lookahead-free construction), letting whoever evaluates redraw boundaries until the verdict fits. There is no statistical power argument: a 30-trade floor per regime with no significance tests or confidence intervals on "median net expectancy" cannot distinguish a 0.25R edge from noise, and the conjunctive win-in-every-regime bar all but guarantees rejection on sampling variation alone. Max drawdown is confounded because the stricter gate mechanically trades less, so lower DD reflects reduced exposure rather than better risk control unless normalized per trade; likewise "falsify if <30 trades" punishes data sparsity as if it were strategy failure. Finally, "do not modify code" contradicts testing a gate whose threshold logic must exist somewhere, and nothing attests that the sealed holdouts were untouched when the 0.25R margin was chosen—without seal-integrity verification, this is silent holdout contamination.
