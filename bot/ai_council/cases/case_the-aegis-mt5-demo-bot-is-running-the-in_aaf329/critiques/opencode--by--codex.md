The proposal risks severe selection and lookahead bias by deriving `validated_states.json` from the same survivor strategies and `validated_opportunities` whose unusually strong metrics motivate the test. “Fill-rate improves” lacks a predefined threshold, sample size, time horizon, cost model, and out-of-sample or sealed-holdout comparison; moreover, testing only whether expectancy turns negative is far too weak to establish benefit. It also omits registry requirements, baseline tests, leakage checks, and explicit confirmation that only research artifacts—not protected live YAML or runtime gating—will be modified.
