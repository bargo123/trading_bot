The "3.0×" multiplier is a magic number with no stated provenance — no sensitivity analysis, no walk-forward estimate of the threshold itself, so it will be tuned until one state crosses p05 > 0, which is textbook multiple-comparison leakage into `validated_states.json`. The falsifiability criterion is nearly vacuous: demanding *at least one* validated state from a firehose generating dozens of candidate configurations guarantees a lucky survivor under null conditions, and "zero validated states" may simply mean there is no edge after costs — the proposal presumes the opposite without evidence. It never specifies the evaluation protocol (data window, out-of-sample holdout, whether the spread is measured at simulated decision time or looked ahead, stop-loss symmetry — a 3×-spread target with tight stops still bleeds on stop-outs) and cites Ed Ponsi instead of any measurement from this system. Finally, it omits the non-negotiable governance steps: no ExperimentRegistry entry, no run through every `promote.py` gate before anything touches champion artifacts — as written, this proposal is not executable under council rules.
