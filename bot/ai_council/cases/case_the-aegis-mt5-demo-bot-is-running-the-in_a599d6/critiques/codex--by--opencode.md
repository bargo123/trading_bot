This proposal burns the sealed holdout as a selection criterion: making the accept/reject call directly against it contaminates it for future challengers, and with no stated sample-size floor, confidence intervals, or multiple-comparison control, a 5% profit-factor edge could be noise from a handful of trades. Key terms are unmeasurable as written — "raises the threshold by 10%" (relative to what baseline; per-strategy or global?) and "increasing maximum drawdown" (which drawdown definition, and with zero tolerance?) — leaving acceptance undecidable. Validation is missing: no walk-forward/in-sample gate before touching the holdout, no re-measurement of champion baselines under identical conditions rather than reusing stale metrics, and no sensitivity check on why 10% is the right shift versus a swept parameter. Finally, the bespoke acceptance rule bypasses governance: promotion must run every gate in `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` via `research_promote_champion.py`, not a council member's improvised criteria.
