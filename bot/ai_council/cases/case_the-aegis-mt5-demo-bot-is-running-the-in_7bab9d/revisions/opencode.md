To address the selection-bias concern, the full variant grid and bucket list are pre-registered here before any further outcome inspection—time-stops at N ∈ {4, 8, 16} bars post-entry and hard loss-caps at 2× and 3× the champion's measured $0.031 avg_win, applied uniformly to all six cosmetic_win_rate buckets plus an untreated control set, with Holm–Bonferroni correction across all tested variants. A data-feasibility audit comes first: since the 2,517-exit outcome log may lack intratrade bar paths, counterfactuals will be reconstructed from point-in-time M1 bars with explicit spread, slippage, and worst-case gap-through-stop execution modeling, and if faithful reconstruction proves impossible the experiment is registered as failed and abandoned rather than approximated. Selection uses nested validation: the in-sample window splits into tuning folds (where the grid is searched under the corrected tests) and a validation fold touched exactly once per surviving variant, followed by a single sealed-holdout pass with no re-tuning or resubmission afterward. Success requires meeting all predefined thresholds simultaneously: sealed-holdout net-of-cost expectancy ≥ +0.01R, PF > 1.0, max drawdown no worse than the incumbent's 5.4%, win-rate decline ≤ 2 percentage points absolute versus 95.7%, ≥ 150 exits per evaluated bucket (sized for power against the observed exit-level variance), and bucket-level stability—positive expectancy in at least five of six buckets with none below −0.02R; failing any gate rejects the challenger, which remains on record un-promoted. The work stays strictly offline throughout: every trial, including failures and the feasibility audit itself, is logged in ExperimentRegistry, protected live YAML and runner configs are never touched, and any promotion proceeds exclusively through the governed `research_promote_champion.py` / `promote.py` pipeline.
