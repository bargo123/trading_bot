A single sealed cycle is far too small a sample to certify a 10% expectancy gain—no significance test, bootstrap, or variance estimate is specified, so you'd be accepting noise dressed as signal. The three acceptance knobs (5% margin band, +10% expectancy, 80% retention) are suspiciously round and unpre-registered, which smells like thresholds reverse-engineered from prior peeking rather than fixed ex ante; and "without worsening max drawdown" is unmeasurable without a tolerance band since drawdown estimates are wildly sample-dependent. There's also no evidence the gate scores are calibrated—the filter presumes proximity-to-threshold implies marginal quality, which is asserted, not validated—and with 80% retention on an unknown baseline trade count, per-bucket sample sizes could be too thin to conclude anything. Safety-wise it's clean (DEMO-only, champion frozen, registry logged), but the experiment must be registered in ExperimentRegistry *before* the run, not after, per house rules.
