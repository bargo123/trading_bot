The “untouched” holdout is vulnerable to overfitting if the same post‑2026‑06‑15 slice is repeatedly inspected or used to choose N/M/K, and a single time split does not establish robustness across regimes. The proposal also assumes the outcome log contains enough intratrade path data to replay time stops, breakeven moves, and realistic execution without lookahead; terminal outcomes alone cannot validate those counterfactual exits. “Win rate stays ≥60%” is an unnecessary optimization target, while costs, gap behavior, simultaneous SL triggers, and the definition of “manual” exits need explicit, testable replay semantics before promotion gates are meaningful.
