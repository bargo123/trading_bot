Replaying exits on the same 2,518 trades used to identify the thresholds invites severe overfitting, especially since “≈0.18 R,” the time limit, and the ≥0.010 R target lack pre-registration or sensitivity analysis. A hard cap is not measurable from exit logs alone without intratrade paths, executable quotes, gap/slippage modeling, and rules for barrier ordering; it may also change the reported 67.5% win rate and invalidate the “same entry set” assumption. The proposal omits walk-forward tuning, untouched holdout size and regime coverage, uncertainty intervals, and comparison against simple baselines. It is safe only if every run is registered and the sealed holdouts remain untouched during design; otherwise repeated evaluation silently contaminates them.
