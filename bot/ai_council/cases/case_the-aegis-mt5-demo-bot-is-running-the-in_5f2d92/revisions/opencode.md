Revised proposal: Before any replay, we will pre-register in the ExperimentRegistry a single frozen parameter set — 0.18 R hard cap, time exit at the median holding time of winning closes, success bar ≥ +0.010 R / PF ≥ 1.05 net-of-cost — together with explicit barrier-ordering rules and conservative worst-fill assumptions at cap breaches (gap/slippage haircut applied since demo logs lack intratrade paths and executable quotes), and we accept up front that the counterfactual win rate may deviate from 67.5% and will be reported as measured. All tuning occurs only via chronological walk-forward splits within the earliest ~70% of the 2,518-trade corpus (regime-stratified); the final 30% window is reserved as an untouched evaluation set, on which we report bootstrap confidence intervals for expectancy/PF and comparisons against three baselines: unchanged exits, time-exit-only, and cap-only. The falsifiable prediction applies solely to that held-out window; if it fails, the candidate is recorded as rejected and the validated-state gate is not touched. Sealed demo holdouts remain untouched throughout design and tuning and are evaluated exactly once, after the pre-registration commit; every run — including sensitivity analyses — is logged in the registry so repeated evaluation cannot silently contaminate anything. If holdout size or regime coverage proves insufficient to certify the result, we record that limitation honestly instead of promoting.
