The 20% invalidated-entry and 10% trade-count thresholds are arbitrary and unpreregistered, and the choice of "two consecutive snapshots" itself is an untested hyperparameter — optimizing any of these on one time-split holdout is textbook selection bias; demand walk-forward or multiple split windows with a minimum sample size, since a 20% delta on sparse entries is easily noise. Key terms are unmeasurable as written: "qualifying snapshot," "invalidated entry," and the expectancy horizon lack operational definitions, and net expectancy plus trade count is insufficient — you must report per-trade distribution shift (tail losses, drawdown) because dropping 10% of trades can silently remove either the worst or the best ones. The "identical signals" framing ignores that delaying entry to a second snapshot changes realized spread/slippage, so costs are not actually identical across arms. Finally, any promotion must flow through the governed `research_promote_champion.py` gate set and `ExperimentRegistry` logging — the proposal names neither, which is where this would silently bypass governance even in DEMO.
