The proposal risks horizon-tuning overfitting: aligning revalidation windows per candidate can implicitly select the horizon that best fits its own historical decay. “Meaningful fraction” and the 10% target lack a pre-registered sample-size, confidence, and multiple-testing standard, so the claimed improvement is not statistically measurable. It also omits temporal leakage controls and a rule for choosing the rolling-window parameters before sealed replay; acceptance-rate changes can make per-trade expectancy look better while reducing deployable opportunity.
