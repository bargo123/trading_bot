The acceptance rule overfits its own evaluation set: tuning a three-condition threshold (net expectancy up, drawdown not worse, ≤25% trade cut) against the same "sealed" holdouts and DEMO logs used to decide acceptance burns the seal and invites selection bias, with no second untouched holdout, no significance testing or confidence intervals, and no minimum effect-size floor — at small trade counts an "improvement" is indistinguishable from noise. Core claims are unmeasurable as written: "positive expected-value margin" has no numeric threshold, "two consecutive scans" is meaningless without a fixed scan cadence (making the filter non-reproducible), and "false-positive rate" lacks any stated ground truth for what a correct entry is. Comparing maximum drawdown across different trade counts is invalid — fewer trades mechanically compress drawdown, so the criterion rewards selectivity rather than risk control — and logged DEMO-paper outcomes cannot be naively replayed because requiring scan-pair confirmation changes the eligible-entry set in ways the historical log never observed (a counterfactual the comparison silently assumes). Finally, "accept it" names no governed path: per AGENTS.md promotion must pass every gate in bot/aegis/research/promote.py via the designated promotion scripts and be registered in the ExperimentRegistry, neither of which the proposal mentions.
