Revised proposal: Replace the static validation window with rolling walk-forward revalidation whose window schedule is pre-registered before any sealed contact — a small fixed grid of lookback lengths defined as multiples of each candidate's own horizon, selected once from training-period decay curves only, with one untouched final holdout evaluated exactly once after the comparison rule is frozen. Sealed slices are stratified across pre-defined regimes with minimum per-slice sample sizes, and the rolling-vs-static verdict uses bootstrap confidence intervals on net-of-cost expectancy deltas with Holm correction across slices and candidates. The hypothesis that rejections reflect short-lived edges is tested directly by measuring realized post-rejection outcome decay of rejected candidates, not inferred from acceptance-rate changes. Replay is strictly as-of (only records timestamped before each decision), overlapping horizons are clustered into non-overlapping executable blocks, and identical runner spread/slippage assumptions are applied to both arms. Success requires total deployable net expectancy (acceptance rate × per-trade expectancy) to rise while CVaR of accepted-trade outcomes and worst clustered loss do not worsen, so abstention-driven per-trade gains that shrink opportunity fail by construction. All existing promotion, broker, DEMO-only, and champion-governance gates remain in force; any failed criterion is recorded honestly in ExperimentRegistry and the champion stays untouched.
