1. The persistence count (two scans) is a free parameter with no stated selection procedure — tuning it against the same sealed holdout until the ≥10% bar clears contaminates the holdout and is textbook multiple-testing overfitting; the criterion also omits any minimum sample size or significance test, so a 10% relative expectancy delta on a thin holdout is indistinguishable from noise.
2. "No increase in trade costs" is unmeasurable as written: costs are runner spread/slippage assumptions, and a double-scan delay changes trade timing and frequency, so per-trade cost parity says nothing about aggregate cost drag — specify cost-normalized expectancy net of realized frequency instead.
3. Validation gaps: no ExperimentRegistry requirement, no regime/subsample breakdown for cases where the first scan fires but the second doesn't, and no analysis of edge decay during the inter-scan wait — delayed entry can systematically buy worse prices, which the raw expectancy comparison would misattribute.
4. "No increase in maximum drawdown" is confounded because the filter trades less (fewer trades mechanically shrink DD), and any acceptance must still route through the full `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` gate suite via `research_promote_champion.py` — the proposal never says so, leaving an ad hoc promotion path open.
