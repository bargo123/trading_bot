The fixed 20%/90% thresholds and 60-day window are arbitrary tuning choices that invite overfitting, especially if many variants were tried before selecting this proposal. “Improves net expectancy” lacks a minimum effect size, confidence interval, significance/multiple-testing correction, and rules for incomplete or correlated trades; 100 trades alone is not adequate validation. It also does not require point-in-time percentile computation, preregistration and ExperimentRegistry recording, walk-forward or multi-period robustness checks, or explicit confirmation that the challenger cannot bypass governed promotion and DEMO-only execution controls.
