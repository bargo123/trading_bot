Spread percentiles may proxy for volatile sessions, news, symbols, or signal quality, so bucket differences cannot establish that spread abstention itself improves EV. “Significant” is undefined and top-quartile samples may be too small or repeatedly sliced, creating selection bias without pre-specified confidence intervals, minimum sample sizes, and time-ordered out-of-sample splits. The proposal also needs validation that the trailing percentile is computed only from information available at entry and remains stable across symbols and regimes; sealed-holdout promotion is appropriate, and no direct safety violation is proposed.
