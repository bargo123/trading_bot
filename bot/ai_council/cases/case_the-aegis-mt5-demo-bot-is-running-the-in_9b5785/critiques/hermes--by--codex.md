The retrospective outcome log is vulnerable to regime and selection bias: “own trailing distribution,” window, minimum sample size, percentile computation, and spread measurement are unspecified, and using the full log risks contaminating the sealed holdout. The rule also lacks pre-registered statistical thresholds, confidence intervals, multiple-comparison control, and a test that aggregate net EV improves after accounting for abstained trade volume, so “remaining buckets stay positive” can be meaningless under low power. No direct safety violation is proposed, but the shadow phase needs explicit isolation from the sole MT5 execution owner and proof that the gate cannot mutate runner, config, or champion state.
