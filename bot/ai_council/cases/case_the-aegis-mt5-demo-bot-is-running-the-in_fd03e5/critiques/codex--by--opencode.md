The 1.5× cost multiplier, 10% expectancy bar, and 0.05 PF tolerance are three unpre-registered free parameters with no stated derivation — tune them post hoc against the same splits and this is a filter-fit-to-backtest machine, so fix and freeze them before any evaluation and register the run in `ExperimentRegistry` (which the proposal never mentions). "Predicted edge < 1.5× modeled cost" is unmeasurable as written: it never specifies the source and calibration status of the edge prediction or whether "modeled" cost means the runner's spread/slippage assumptions or empirically realized fills, and aggregate net-expectancy improvement can mask catastrophic single-fold regressions — require per-fold significance (e.g., bootstrap CI excluding zero) plus a minimum trade count per bucket. Repeatedly hitting the sealed holdout with successive challengers erodes its seal; there's no multiple-testing accounting or statement that promotion runs only through the governed `promote.py` gates rather than this ad-hoc "accept" clause. Finally, +25% is a single arbitrary stress point — sweep at least 1.25–3× cost, since a filter whose edge evaporates at 2× cost is curve-fit to today's spread.
