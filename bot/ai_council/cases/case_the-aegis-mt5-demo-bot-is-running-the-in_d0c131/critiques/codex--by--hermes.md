The proposal never operationalizes "quote stability" or why three consecutive scans is the right threshold — an unjustified magic number invites a post-hoc parameter search, which is the classic overfitting vector here. Its success criteria are statistically vacuous: ≥10% fewer invalidations is meaningless without a minimum sample size and power analysis (a short DEMO window may contain only a handful of invalidations), and "expectancy reduced by ≤5%" is unstable if baseline expectancy is near zero; neither threshold carries a significance test or confidence interval. Validation is underspecified — a single fixed holdout cannot distinguish signal from regime noise, and the design must state whether gate variants run concurrently on the same tape or sequentially (sequential comparison is confounded by market conditions), plus how the arms' non-overlapping trade sets are compared fairly. Safety-wise it correctly promotes nothing, but it must pre-register the exact gate definition, thresholds, and stopping rules in the ExperimentRegistry *before* the first scan, and confirm any gate-variant runs only through the designated DEMO execution owner with a defined mid-experiment abort condition.
