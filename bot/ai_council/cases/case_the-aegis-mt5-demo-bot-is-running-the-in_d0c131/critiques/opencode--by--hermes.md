The challenger's core premise is statistically weak: two "consecutive" signals from the same state are likely autocorrelated products of one regime rather than independent confirmations, and "validated-state" is never operationally defined, so the filter plausibly just thins trade count while adding no real evidence. Evaluating only on the sealed holdout with no stated minimum sample size, significance test, or penalty for repeatedly challenging the champion against that same seal converts the seal into a reusable tuning set — precisely the multiple-comparison leakage sealed holdouts exist to prevent. The promotion bar is unmeasurable as written: "improves net expectancy" and "does not worsen maximum drawdown or tail-loss" carry no margin, tolerance, or confidence-interval/bootstrap requirement, and tail-loss estimates on a deliberately thinned trade population will be mostly noise. Finally, the workflow shows no in-sample/walk-forward development phase before touching the seal and no commitment to ExperimentRegistry recording, skipping the governed validation sequence the lab requires.
