Using each candidate’s own horizon is post-selection and can cherry-pick the maturity point that maximizes expectancy, contaminating the sealed holdout and invalidating comparison with the fixed gate. “Per-candidate-horizon economics” is asserted rather than specified or independently validated, and the proposal lacks preregistered horizon-selection rules, sample-size/power analysis, confidence intervals, and multiplicity correction. Session/symbol concentration is an inadequate regime guard; it should also test unseen time periods, costs, turnover, and robustness to nearby horizons, with immutable holdout access enforced.
