A 100-trade replay and a single 4-hour forward-return comparison are too weak to establish regime-specific causality or rule out selection bias, especially after inspecting rejection logs. The proposed 15% threshold is arbitrary, and “high-volume periods” lacks a pre-registered, measurable definition. Require walk-forward, sealed holdout validation with net costs and drawdown/tail-risk gates before any gate change; DEMO-derived behavior must not be treated as evidence for live deployment.
