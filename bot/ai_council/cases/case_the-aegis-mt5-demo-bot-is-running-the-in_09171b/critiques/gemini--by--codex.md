Rejected signals are a biased, non-tradable counterfactual, so comparing their 4-hour returns with accepted trades cannot establish that the gate caused missed opportunity, especially without spread/slippage and execution modeling. “High-volume,” “high-probability,” and the arbitrary 15% variance increase are undefined and invite regime cherry-picking, while a 100-trade replay is underpowered and highly vulnerable to overfitting. No untouched out-of-sample, walk-forward, statistical uncertainty, or pre-registered acceptance criteria is specified, and changing a gate based on demo logs risks violating the governed-promotion process unless fully registry-recorded and passed through all research gates.
