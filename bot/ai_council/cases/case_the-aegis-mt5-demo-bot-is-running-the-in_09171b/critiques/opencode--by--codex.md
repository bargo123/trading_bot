Two consecutive confirmations are an arbitrary, easily overfit rule and may selectively suppress signals without demonstrating better risk-adjusted performance. A 10% sealed-holdout expectancy gain with unchanged maximum drawdown and 100 signals is insufficient without confidence intervals, statistical significance, costs/slippage, regime and temporal robustness, and multiple-testing correction. The proposal also omits explicit enforcement of the existing promotion gates and does not define “validated-state,” “eligible,” or how drawdown and expectancy are measured.
