Analyze the validated-state gate's rejection logs from the MT5 DEMO environment to determine if high-probability mean-reversion signals are being filtered during peak London-NY overlap sessions. By comparing the 4-hour forward returns of trades rejected by the current gate versus those accepted, the next research cycle will test if the variance threshold is over-optimized for low-volatility regimes. We propose a falsifiable experiment to increase the gate’s allowable variance by 15% specifically during high-volume periods to capture missed volatility. Success will be measured by a 5% improvement in the Sharpe ratio across a 100-trade backtest replay using the intelligent firehose logic.
