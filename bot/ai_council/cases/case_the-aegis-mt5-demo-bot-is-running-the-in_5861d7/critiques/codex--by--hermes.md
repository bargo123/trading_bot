The rolling 75th-percentile rule is underspecified exactly where it matters — window length, trailing-only computation, tick- vs bar-basis — leaving both lookahead risk and free parameter room to tune against the holdout, and no sensitivity scan across percentiles showing 75 wasn't itself fitted. "Improves expectancy and drawdown" is unmeasurable as written: no minimum effect size, no significance testing, and no requirement that the filter fires often enough on the holdout for the suppressed-entry sample to carry any statistical power. The evaluation conflates per-trade quality with portfolio outcome — suppressing trades can lift expectancy while cutting absolute net PnL, so the aggregate net-of-cost delta versus the unchanged baseline must be reported alongside per-trade metrics. Nothing confirms the holdout was genuinely untouched by prior champion-selection work, that the run is registered in the ExperimentRegistry, or that a walk-forward check guards against the spread distribution shifting out from under the fitted percentile after a broker/session change.
