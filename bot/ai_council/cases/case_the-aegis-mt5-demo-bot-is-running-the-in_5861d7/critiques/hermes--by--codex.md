Ten DEMO trading days and ≥200 scans are an underpowered, regime-dependent sample, with no predeclared significance level, power analysis, or correction for repeated/threshold searches. Raw halted-versus-passing means are confounded by setup type, symbol, time, spread, and market regime; without blinded, matched, label-safe evaluation and confidence intervals, “protective” is not identifiable. The counterfactual must be generated strictly after the decision using frozen cost assumptions and remain isolated from execution; any relaxed challenger must still undergo full out-of-sample, sealed-holdout, risk, and promotion gates rather than being justified by this single observational test.
