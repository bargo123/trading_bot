The rolling-window length and the 75th-percentile cutoff are unspecified free parameters; if they're tuned with more than one look at the "sealed" holdout, the holdout is burned, so they must be fixed a priori on a development period, with the holdout touched once under a pre-registered effect size and significance threshold instead of the unmeasurable "improves expectancy and drawdown." Suppressing entries cuts trade count and exposure, so raw expectancy/drawdown comparisons are confounded — require exposure-normalized metrics and a minimum holdout trade count, otherwise you're rewarding trading less, not trading better. The proposal never commits to ExperimentRegistry logging, defines neither "validated-state gate" concretely (the full `bot/aegis/research/promote.py` gate suite) nor what "robustness checks" entails, and ignores non-stationarity: a spread distribution fitted on one regime can silently become the wrong filter in the next.
