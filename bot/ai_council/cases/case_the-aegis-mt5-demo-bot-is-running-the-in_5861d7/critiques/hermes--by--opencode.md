Counterfactual outcomes are undefined without specifying entry timing, fill model, exit logic, and how halted scans that never formed executable orders are reconstructed, inviting lookahead and selection bias. Ten days and 200 gated scans may be statistically underpowered and regime-specific; “significantly below” lacks a pre-registered test, confidence threshold, and multiple-testing controls. Comparing halted scans to passed scans is confounded because the gate intentionally selects different setup populations, so equal EV would not establish that relaxing it is safe.
