1. Evaluating the accept/reject decision on the sealed holdout unseals it: every ablation scored against the same holdout is another multiple-testing draw, so repeated proposals will eventually pass by chance — there's no correction, no pre-registered effect-size threshold ("improves" by any epsilon counts), and no commitment to re-log the attempt in ExperimentRegistry per repo rules.
2. "Max drawdown does not increase" is a point estimate on a single price path — noise alone can flip it either way, and the baseline must be re-simulated on the identical window/costs for the comparison to mean anything, which the proposal doesn't state.
3. The 15-minute value is a magic number with no sensitivity sweep (10/20/30/60), inviting overfitting to whatever the holdout happens to reward; and the ≥80% trade-count floor plus expectancy-only acceptance can mask a strategy that deletes its best trades — total net PnL or profit factor must be checked too, since higher expectancy on fewer trades can still be worse in absolute terms.
4. Safety gap: as written the ablation implies placing actual MT5 DEMO entries to measure the cooldown, but research/probe components may never call order_send — the ablation must be validated purely in simulation through the recorded execution path, with any live-gate change left to the governed runner after promotion gates pass.
