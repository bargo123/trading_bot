Proposal: Using the newly reconciled broker-triggered-exit telemetry, measure per-trade execution slippage on the DEMO outcome log (signal-time quote vs. actual fill, plus stop-loss fill vs. modeled stop) across the next ~200 closed firehose trades, and test the hypothesis that slippage-adjusted expectancy is materially below the champion's modeled expectancy (e.g., median adverse slippage exceeding one spread-width reduces mean R by ≥0.05). This is falsifiable: if measured slippage-adjusted expectancy stays within the model's confidence band, the hypothesis is rejected and no change is warranted; if it fails, register a challenger via ExperimentRegistry that adds a spread/liquidity-aware entry gate (abstain when live spread exceeds trailing median + k·MAD) and evaluate it against the sealed holdout under the existing promote.py gates. This follows Harris's "Trading and Exchanges" point that realized performance reflects the price of immediacy, not just signal quality, and Chan's emphasis on validating net-of-costs assumptions against real fills rather than backtest quotes. No champion edits, config changes, or order flow are proposed — measurement only, feeding the next governed promotion cycle.
