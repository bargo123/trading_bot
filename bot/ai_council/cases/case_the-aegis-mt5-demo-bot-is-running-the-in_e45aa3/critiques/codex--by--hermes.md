The core flaw is attributional confounding: applying the timestamp gate changes the trade population itself, so comparing post-filter expectancy/PF against the −$0.01119 baseline measures survivorship from censoring bad fills, not decision improvement — the design needs a same-population ablation (gate on/off over identical windows) before any expectancy claim is meaningful. The acceptance criteria are incomplete and partly unmeasurable: only future-quote events are gated to zero while the 12,463 stale events carry no threshold, and "zero future events" ignores benign clock/NTP skew between client and broker feeds, which can make the invariant either unreachable or achievable only by disabling the feed — the proposal must define detection methodology, an epsilon, and root-cause attribution. Overfitting and leakage risk are high: PF > 1.0 on roughly 200 costed trades has a confidence interval wide enough to be indistinguishable from noise, the thresholds were selected using the same audit statistics they will be validated on, and no holdout split, multiple-comparison control across competing gates, or out-of-sample replication is specified. Validation gaps include no accounting for good trades falsely rejected by the gate (the true cost side of the ledger), undefined behavior when the sample falls below 200, and no ExperimentRegistry entry despite the lab's mandatory-recording rule — though to its credit the proposal stays within DEMO shadow/replay and violates no MT5 safety constraint.
