The proposed ~200-trade sample is likely underpowered and selection-biased, while “materially,” the confidence band, spread-width threshold, and ≥0.05R effect size are undefined or arbitrary. Signal-time quotes, fills, and modeled stops may not be timestamp- or venue-comparable, and DEMO execution telemetry cannot establish live-market slippage without validating broker, latency, and liquidity representativeness. The plan also risks tuning \(k\) and the gate against the sealed holdout, lacks preregistered windows and multiple-testing controls, and should not claim “no safety change” until confirming the measurement path is strictly read-only and cannot invoke order execution.
