The trailing 80th-percentile threshold is underspecified and risks overfitting to symbol, session, and lookback choices; it must be frozen before testing and computed strictly from information available at each signal. “DEMO-equivalent” data is unmeasurable without documented spread, rejection, latency, and fill distributions, while expectancy plus a 70% trade-count floor ignores confidence intervals, drawdown, tail losses, and regime robustness. No untouched out-of-sample or forward validation, multiple-testing correction, or explicit experiment-registry record is required, so the apparent improvement could be selection noise despite the promotion safeguard.
