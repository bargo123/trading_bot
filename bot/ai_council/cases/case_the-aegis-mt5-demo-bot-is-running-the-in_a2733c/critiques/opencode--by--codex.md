Session-specific thresholds create multiple-comparison and sparse-sample overfitting risk, especially if session boundaries or cutoffs are tuned against the sealed holdout. “Aggregate net expectancy improves” is unmeasurable without predeclared horizons, confidence intervals, and per-session floors; aggregation can conceal a harmful session-level degradation. The 20% trade-count allowance is arbitrary and may mask abstention-driven selection bias, while the proposal does not require walk-forward validation, independent replication, or explicit DEMO-only/no-order safeguards for evaluation.
