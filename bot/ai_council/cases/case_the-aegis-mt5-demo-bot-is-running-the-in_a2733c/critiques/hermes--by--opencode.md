The claim that a $0.10 cap “alone” flips PF above 1.0 is unvalidated: replaying historical exits cannot assume fills at the cap under spread, slippage, gaps, and tick sparsity. With complete tick evidence for only 47 trades, MFE/MAE validation is materially underpowered and any cap tuned on all 2,802 reconciled exits risks selection bias or label leakage. A tighter stop may lower the stated unchanged win rate and alter trade duration, re-entry behavior, and tail outcomes, so these must be measured on sealed out-of-sample data before changing governed execution geometry.
