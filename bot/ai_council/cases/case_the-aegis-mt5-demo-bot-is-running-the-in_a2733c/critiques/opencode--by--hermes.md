The proposal grants six-plus unconstrained degrees of freedom (separate sample and net-EV floors per session) with no statement that thresholds are frozen pre-registration before touching the sealed holdouts — tune them after observing holdout performance and this is selection on the test set, compounded by three uncorrected chances to pass. Its acceptance metrics are maskable at the aggregate level: total net expectancy can rise while one session (typically Asia) turns sharply negative, and "no increase in maximum drawdown" plus a ≤20% trade-count cut are not comparable-basis criteria, since a shrunken, re-weighted trade population mechanically alters drawdown and guts the statistical power behind the very minimum-sample gates being imposed. Neither the acceptance rule nor the gate spec defines how sessions are assigned (broker-server time vs UTC, DST handling), so "session-conditioned" is not yet a measurable claim, and there is no commitment to register the experiment in `ExperimentRegistry` as AGENTS.md rule 4 mandates. The champion-preservation clause is correct, but as written the proposal is unfalsifiable rather than safe: require pre-registered thresholds, per-session non-degradation bounds, a defined session clock, and registry entry before it goes to evaluation.
