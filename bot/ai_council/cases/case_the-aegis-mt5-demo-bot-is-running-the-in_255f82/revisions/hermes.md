Hypothesis unchanged: a subset of states passing the validated-state gate realizes materially less net EV than predicted, indicating validation overfit rather than execution deterioration. All decision thresholds are pre-registered in the ExperimentRegistry before any measurement: a minimum of 100 sealed trades per analysis cell (sparser deciles are pooled upward until the minimum is met), effect size defined as mean(realized − predicted net EV) below a fixed bps-per-trade floor set at registration, two-sided α=0.05 with Holm correction across all tested cells, and inference via moving-block bootstrap over time-ordered, non-overlapping trade cohorts so serial dependence and position overlap cannot inflate significance. Calibration is leakage-controlled by construction: a predicted-EV-bin → expected-realized-net-EV map is fitted only on trades closed in an early window that predates all current-gate decisions, frozen, then scored exactly once on a strictly later, previously untouched holdout window under the same prospective sealing rule, so sealed-only selection is identical across bins and windows; any observed shortfall is decomposed into prediction error versus realized spread/slippage drift by re-netting each outcome against its own logged fill costs, so worsening execution conditions cannot masquerade as validation overfit. Confirmation requires the frozen map's top-bin deficit to exceed the pre-registered effect floor and the pre-computed minimum detectable effect at the achieved sample size, in which case bin-level abstention advances solely as a challenger through the normal governed promotion path with a predefined recalibration cadence; any other outcome — including all bins inside their bands — is recorded verbatim as a null or negative result in the ExperimentRegistry and the gate remains unchanged. As originally scoped, this requires no new market data, no execution-path changes, and no champion modification during measurement.
