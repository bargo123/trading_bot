Trailing, state-level bootstrap bands are undefined without minimum sample sizes, temporal ordering, and multiple-testing controls; sparse deciles will manufacture apparent breaches. “Systematic shortfall” and “approximately zero” lack pre-registered thresholds, and comparing outcomes to an unspecified validation-window prediction risks leakage and regime-mixed calibration. It also ignores spread/slippage changes and selection bias from only sealed trades, so a detected deficit may reflect execution conditions rather than validated-state overfit.
