The test risks severe selection bias and overfitting because it reuses outcomes and prediction bins that may have influenced the validated-state gate, then proposes decile-specific abstention after inspecting the same data. Bootstrap confidence bands are unjustified without handling serial dependence, regime changes, overlapping trades, costs, and multiple-bin testing; small or uneven deciles can make “systematic shortfall” unmeasurable. It also lacks a truly untouched holdout and a predefined effect-size, sample-size, and recalibration rule, so neither confirmation nor the claimed null result would establish deployable calibration.
