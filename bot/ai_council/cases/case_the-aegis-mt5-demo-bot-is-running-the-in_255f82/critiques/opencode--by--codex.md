“Validated-state confirmation” and “false start” are undefined, making the test non-reproducible and vulnerable to post-hoc labeling. A single sealed replay comparison is insufficient without time/regime splits, confidence intervals, and controls for serial dependence and multiple tuning attempts; the 70% trade-count threshold is arbitrary and may accept a lower-quality strategy. The proposal also does not specify an unchanged execution/risk configuration or explicitly prohibit deployment/promotion from this result, leaving a governance and safety gap.
