The 1.5× spread-plus-slippage threshold is an arbitrary, potentially overfit cutoff unless fixed before evaluation and justified on independent data. A single sealed MT5 DEMO outcome set cannot establish robustness; require chronological walk-forward validation, multiple regimes, and confidence intervals for expectancy and tail-loss comparisons. “Estimated” transaction costs and “no increase” in tail-loss rate are underspecified, and the proposal omits explicit checks that the filter cannot alter governed risk, execution ownership, or DEMO-only safety gates.
