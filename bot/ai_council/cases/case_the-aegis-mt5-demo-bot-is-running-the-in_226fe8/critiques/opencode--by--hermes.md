The 1.5x multiplier is an unjustified magic number — a post-hoc hyperparameter with no sensitivity analysis, and repeatedly testing new candidates against the same sealed holdout is textbook data snooping: each challenger re-peeked at the seal makes "higher net expectancy" reachable by chance alone absent any multiple-comparison correction or fresh out-of-sample window. Both acceptance criteria are unmeasurable as written: the filter gates on its own self-estimated edge (circular, miscalibration invisible), and "no increase in tail-loss rate" specifies neither a tail metric (CVaR at what quantile?), an equivalence margin, nor a significance test — on thin DEMO samples underpowered silence gets read as "no regression." Stricter abstention also mechanically inflates per-trade expectancy while shrinking trade count, so the proposal must state a minimum-trade/power floor and evaluate aggregate net PnL, not per-trade figures, or it will promote a do-nothing strategy. Finally, it omits walk-forward or purged validation entirely and ignores that DEMO-recorded spreads and slippage are systematically optimistic versus real liquidity, so a threshold calibrated on them won't transfer.
