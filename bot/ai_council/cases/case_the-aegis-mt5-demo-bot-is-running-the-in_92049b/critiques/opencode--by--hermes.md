The core flaw is a counterfactual gap: sealed DEMO logs were generated under the *current* gate, so entries the two-confirm rule would have blocked never exist in the data — you cannot compute the challenger's adverse-excursion rate on a population the incumbent already filtered, and any retrospective reconstruction risks lookahead and selection bias; the honest test requires forward shadow-logging of both gates in parallel. "Validated-state confirmation" is undefined (tick? bar close? which validator?), making the primary metric unmeasurable as stated. The 15%/5% adoption thresholds are arbitrary with no power analysis or multiplicity correction — classic threshold-tuning overfit risk — and there's no significance test, regime/out-of-sample split, or acknowledgment that a delayed entry is a *different* trade (different fill price and spread), violating the "identical signals" premise. Finally, "adopt only if" sidesteps governed promotion: any change must still route through `promote.py` gates and the ExperimentRegistry, which the proposal omits entirely.
