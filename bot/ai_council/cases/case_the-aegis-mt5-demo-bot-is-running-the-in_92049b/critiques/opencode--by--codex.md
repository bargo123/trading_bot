The 15% adverse-excursion and 5% expectancy thresholds are arbitrary and invite threshold overfitting without confidence intervals, power analysis, or correction for repeated experiments. “Validated-state confirmation,” “false start,” and adverse-excursion measurement are undefined, while the second confirmation changes entry timing and therefore spread, slippage, fill probability, and exposure; identical signals do not ensure a fair counterfactual. The proposal lacks preregistered holdout validation, regime/time-split robustness checks, minimum sample requirements, and explicit governed promotion safeguards restricting any deployment to the approved MT5 DEMO path.
