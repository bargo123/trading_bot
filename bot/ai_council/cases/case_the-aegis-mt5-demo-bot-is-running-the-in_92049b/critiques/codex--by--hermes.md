This proposal commits sequential multiple testing: it re-runs an already-tuned filter against ever-newer "fresh" sealed windows until one passes, which inflates the family-wise false-positive rate with every retry and requires an explicit correction (e.g., White's reality check or a pre-committed attempt budget), not just another roll of the dice. The gates are underpowered and partly unmeasurable — the −0.0345→+0.0115 swing could rest on a handful of trades with no stated minimum sample size or power analysis, "positive net expectancy" and "PF > 1" accept noise-level magnitudes with no floors, and "sampled losses" is undefined. Beating the unfiltered firehose is a hollow bar when that baseline itself carries negative costed expectancy; the null should be a pre-registered minimum absolute edge, frozen in the experiment registry before the holdout unseals, with costs matching the runner's live spread/slippage assumptions. Given the prior bootstrap failure, the burden of proof is inverted: unless the new window was untouched by all prior tuning iterations and the acceptance thresholds were committed before any of its data existed, this is holdout reuse dressed up as validation — reject as specified.
