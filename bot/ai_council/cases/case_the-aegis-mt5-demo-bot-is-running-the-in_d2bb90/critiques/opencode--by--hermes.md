The 25th–75th percentile band is an unjustified free parameter — evaluating exactly one hand-picked configuration against the sealed holdout all but guarantees post-hoc threshold tweaking on the same sealed data after a marginal result, which contaminates the holdout; demand a pre-registered threshold-sensitivity grid logged in ExperimentRegistry (AGENTS.md rule 4) before any single-config verdict counts. The acceptance criteria are unmeasurable as written: no minimum effect size or significance bar for the expectancy/profit-factor deltas, and because the filter mechanically cuts exposure, a non-increased max drawdown may simply reflect trading less rather than better signal — normalize drawdown per unit of exposure or time in-market. Validation is also incomplete: there is no walk-forward or regime-stratified out-of-sample check, no explicit guarantee that trailing percentiles use only completed bars (point-in-time, no lookahead at the decision timestamp), and no frozen, pinned definition of the "validated-state baseline" it is diffed against. No hard safety violation since this stays research-side, but note its ad-hoc reject rule must not substitute for the governed promotion gates in bot/aegis/research/promote.py — passing this filter's criteria confers no promotion rights.
