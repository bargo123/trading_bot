Proposal: Run an execution-fidelity audit of the validated-state gate using the existing journal plus outcome logs — join every closed trade to its firing-decision record and label it by (regime, structure, session, side), then compute costed expectancy separately for trades matching the two validated states (range/trend × asia × sell) versus all others. The measured evidence motivates exactly this: the failure audit shows the validated set contains only two asia-sell states (validate expectancy +3.15R and +1.40R), yet realized results over 2,404 closed trades are net-negative (PF 0.751, expectancy −0.011, net −26.9) with a fired-trade mix dominated by buy-side and non-Asia sessions (e.g., EURUSD-buy 202 fires) that cannot belong to the validated set. Falsifiable prediction: the exact-validated-state subset shows positive net expectancy with bootstrap p05 above 0 while the remainder is negative; if instead the matched subset is also non-positive, the offline validation does not survive real spread/slippage and the cost assumption (0.3 pips) in ml_pipeline selection is too optimistic. If confirmed, the next-cycle candidate is tightening the runtime gate so only journal-verified validated-state contexts reach the OMS, converting roughly the 23,805 "no_validated_strategy_model" abstentions into consistent behavior rather than mixed-path firing. This follows López de Prado's "Advances in Financial Machine Learning" guidance that a backtested edge must be shown to survive under the actual labeling and cost regime it will trade in, not an assumed one.
