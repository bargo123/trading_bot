“Validated-state” and the confirmation timing are undefined, inviting post-hoc tuning and possible label leakage around bar boundaries. Net expectancy improvement without a predefined minimum effect size, confidence interval, trade-count threshold, or regime-by-regime breakdown is insufficient validation and can promote noise. Drawdown “does not worsen” is also unmeasurable without a fixed metric and tolerance, while the extra-bar delay must be assessed for missed trades and changed fill assumptions.
