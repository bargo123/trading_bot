Per-state calibration can overfit sparse or non-stationary outcome slices; the proposal lacks minimum sample sizes, confidence intervals, and multiple-testing controls for selecting thresholds. “Lifecycle/regime state” and the exact calibration protocol are unspecified, making leakage and reproducibility difficult to rule out. A single sealed holdout can itself be tuned against, so acceptance needs pre-registered state definitions and repeated walk-forward validation before any governed promotion.
