The fixed 30-minute and 0.25R thresholds are arbitrary hyperparameters that invite overfitting, especially if “non-trending validated-state” is not precisely defined and independently frozen before testing. A single sealed-holdout comparison lacks uncertainty estimates, minimum sample requirements, and correction for prior candidate searches; profit factor and maximum drawdown can be highly unstable. The acceptance rule also permits economically weak results if trade count falls by up to 15%, and does not require robustness across instruments, regimes, or time periods.
